QUOTE
What you call 'yourself' is fear. The 'you' is born out of fear; it lives in fear, functions in fear and dies in fear.

CAPTION
Most of us treat fear as something that happens to us, a visitor we would like to send away. U.G. Krishnamurti turned this around. He did not say that you have fear. He said that what you call yourself is fear. The talk of overcoming fear is fear's own game, and he said we do not want it to end. He offered no method and no comfort here, and he would not let the sentence become a new belief. He offered nothing to do with it.

SOURCE
U.G. Krishnamurti, No Way Out
