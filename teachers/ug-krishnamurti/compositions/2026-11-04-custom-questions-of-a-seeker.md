# Questions of a Seeker

The questions marked Q. are written for this page and belong to no one in particular.
Every answer is U.G. Krishnamurti's own, quoted exactly, with its source beneath it.

Q. I have been looking for enlightenment for many years. What is it?

> God or Enlightenment is the ultimate pleasure, uninterrupted happiness. No such thing exists.

— *No Way Out*, Ch. 1, The Unrational Philosophy of U.G. Krishnamurti

Q. Surely liberation is something more than happiness?

> Transformation, moksha, liberation, and all that stuff are just variations on the same theme: permanent happiness.

— *No Way Out*

Q. What did you find at the end of your own search?

> The search ends with the realization that there is no such thing as enlightenment.

— *The Mystique of Enlightenment*

Q. Is that realization a kind of enlightenment, then?

> To realize that there is no enlightenment at all is enlightenment.

— *The Mystique of Enlightenment*, Part Four

Q. Can you give it to me?

> I have nothing to give; you have nothing to take.

— *The Mystique of Enlightenment*, Part Four

Q. Should I look for another teacher who can help me?

> If anyone thinks he can help you, he will inevitably mislead you…

— *The Mystique of Enlightenment*

Q. What about the true self I am trying to realize?

> there is no self to realize

— *The Mystique of Enlightenment*, Part One

Q. Will I at least come to experience what lies beyond?

> …the beyond can never be experienced by you; it is when the 'you' is not there.

— *The Mystique of Enlightenment*, Part Two

Q. What is your own state like?

> This state is a physical condition of your being. It is not some kind of psychological mutation.

— *The Mystique of Enlightenment*

Q. People speak of it as bliss. Was it?

> I call it 'calamity' because from the point of view of one who thinks this is something fantastic, blissful and full of beatitude, love, or ecstasy, this is physical torture.

— *The Mystique of Enlightenment*, Part One

Q. What brought it about?

> The natural state is acausal: it just happens.

— *The Mystique of Enlightenment*

Q. Then why do you speak to people at all?

> I am only interested in describing this state, in clearing away the occultation and mystification in which those people in the 'holy business' have shrouded the whole thing.

— *The Mystique of Enlightenment*, Part One

Q. I still feel that I need to understand.

> There is nothing to understand — that is the understanding I am talking about.

— *The Mystique of Enlightenment*, Part Four

Q. May I write down what you have said and pass it on?

> My teaching, if that is the word you want to use, has no copyright. You are free to reproduce, distribute, interpret, misinterpret, distort, garble, do what you like...

— *The Mystique of Enlightenment*, front matter
