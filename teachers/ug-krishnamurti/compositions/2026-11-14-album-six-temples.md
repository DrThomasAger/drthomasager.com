# Six Temples

Six Suno songs made only of U.G. Krishnamurti's recorded words, each set in the sound of an Indian enlightenment tradition and its deity. Every sung line is his, copied word for word from a verified quotation and checked by script; no line is the writer's. Each song's lyrics are exactly 4,444 characters and each style block exactly 1,111 characters of instrumental keywords, so each can be pasted into Suno as it stands. Each was composed in parallel from the same Smile prompt that `recompose.py compose --format suno` makes, with this album's asking added.

In a second pass every song was recut from his words for rhyme, between line endings and inside lines, measured with the CMU pronouncing dictionary, and given a mantra chorus of its own, a short run of his words chanted in repetition; the lyrics stayed exactly 4,444 characters. Each song's page carries its style and lyrics with a button to copy each for Suno, and its sources, every sung line beside the id of the quotation it comes from, and its rhymes, the scheme and every rhyme found.
