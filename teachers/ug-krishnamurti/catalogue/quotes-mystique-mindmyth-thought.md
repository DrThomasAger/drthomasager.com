## A. THE MYSTIQUE OF ENLIGHTENMENT (1982, ed. Rodney Arms)

1. VERBATIM: "To be yourself requires extraordinary intelligence." (slug continues "…You are blessed with that intelligence…" [fragment]) — Goodreads quote 585068; Goodreads work 52201; Wikiquote (listed under Mystique). Also found as transcript on inner-quest.org/UG_Words.htm (native intelligence vs. acquired intellect). Some quote sites attach it to No Way Out.
   https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/585068-to-be-yourself-requires-extraordinary-intelligence-you-are-blessed-with

2. VERBATIM: "To realize that there is *no* enlightenment at all *is* enlightenment." Followed by Q: "So there is enlightenment!" — UG: "If you want to call it that." — Wikisource Part Four (kwize says Part Three — see conflicts).
   https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Mystique_of_Enlightenment/Part_Four ; https://kwize.com/r/U.+G.+Krishnamurti,+The+Mystique+of+Enlightenment/

3. VERBATIM: "Enlightenment (if there is any such thing as enlightenment) is not an experience at all." — Wikisource (Mystique).

4. VERBATIM: "Unquestioned action is morality." Part of the Q&A passage beginning "What is morality? It is not the following of enjoined [rules]…" [fragment]; the passage also says questioning one's actions "is destroying the expression of life" [paraphrase] and describes a man who lets life act "without the protective movement of thought" [paraphrase]. — Goodreads quote 984886; Goodreads author quotes 30268; thequotearchive.com. Goodreads has one listing crediting Mind Is a Myth (conflict).
   https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/984886-what-is-morality-it-is-not-the-following-of-enjoined

5. VERBATIM (Wikiquote fragment): "…discovered that there is no such thing as enlightenment at all…" ; rest [paraphrase]: so the question of whether a particular person is enlightened does not arise. — Wikiquote; awakeningtoreality.com/2018/10/ug-krishnamurti-mystique-of.html

6. VERBATIM fragment: "there is no self to realize" — Wikiquote (Mystique section). Quotlr renders the longer version [paraphrase]: the realization that there is no self to realize comes as a shattering blow, hits like a thunderbolt.

7. VERBATIM fragment (on gurus/claimants): "a bunch of exploiters, thriving on the gullibility of the people" — Wikiquote (Mystique).

8. VERBATIM fragment (opening copyright statement): "…has no copyright. You are free to reproduce, distribute, interpret, misinterpret, distort, garble, do what you like…" — Wikiquote (Mystique section).

9. VERBATIM fragment: "…nature had been allowed to go on in its own way, everybody would have become a unique flower." — Wikiquote (Mystique).

10. VERBATIM: "I call these events a calamity because from the point of view of one who thinks this is something fantastic, blissful, full of love, ecstasy and all that kind of a thing, this is physical torture." and "Not a calamity to me but to those who have an image that something marvelous is going to happen." — Part One; https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Mystique_of_Enlightenment/Part_One ; https://jhana8.com/ug-krishnamurti-and-the-calamity-experience/

11. VERBATIM: "This state is a physical condition of your being. It is not some kind of psychological mutation." — Mystique (Wikisource/jhana8).

12. VERBATIM: "The natural state is acausal: it just happens." — Mystique (Wikisource; also quoted in publisher copy).

13. VERBATIM: "The things that had astonished me that week had become permanent fixtures." — Part One (Wikisource).

14. VERBATIM fragment (biographical, Part One): "By 1961 my money was finished…" and "…a tremendous upheaval…" which he later called "the calamity"; "in his forty-ninth year" [paraphrase]. — Wikisource Part One.

15. VERBATIM fragment: entering the natural state "is like a nuclear explosion" — quoted in Simon & Schuster description. https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-Mystique-of-Enlightenment/U-G-Krishnamurti/9780971078611

16. VERBATIM: "The search ends with the realization that there is no such thing as enlightenment." — azquotes 610426 (attributed to Mystique). https://www.azquotes.com/quote/610426

17. VERBATIM: "There is no power outside of man. Man has created God out of fear. So the problem is fear and not God." — azquotes 811994 (attrib. Mystique); quotefancy 923742; hindu-blog ("man has created God out of fear"). https://quotefancy.com/quote/923742/...

18. VERBATIM: "We don't want to be free from fear. All that we want to do is to play games with it and talk about freeing ourselves from fear." — quotefancy 923760; azquotes (attrib. Mystique). https://quotefancy.com/quote/923760/...

19. VERBATIM fragment: "If anyone thinks he can help you, he will inevitably mislead you…" ; continuation [paraphrase]: and the less phony / the more powerful and enlightened he seems, the more misery and mischief he will create for you. — azquotes (attrib. Mystique); quotefancy; quotlr.

20. VERBATIM fragment: "There is no such thing as experience here. You seem…" — azquotes 934218. https://www.azquotes.com/quote/934218

21. VERBATIM fragment: "We are not created for any grander purpose than the…" — azquotes 487332. https://www.azquotes.com/quote/487332

22. VERBATIM fragment: "Nature is interested in only two things--to survive and to…" — azquotes 949641. https://www.azquotes.com/quote/949641

23. Editor's words, NOT UG (Rodney Arms intro / publisher): "one can do nothing to attain the natural state. In fact, any movement towards it separates one from it." — Goodreads/Google Books description. Also Goodreads 52201 lists quotes on "the beyond" and "the natural man" [titles only, text not returned].

## B. MIND IS A MYTH (1987/88, ed. Terry Newland)

Chapter titles recovered (kwize/quotepark): Ch.1 "The Certainty That Blasts Everything"; Ch.3 "Not Knowing Is Your Natural State"; Ch.4 "There Is Nothing To Understand"; "Hope Is for Tomorrow"; "We Have Created This Jungle Society"; "The Body as a Crucible".

24. VERBATIM: "Thought can never capture the movement of life, it is much too slow. It is like lightning and thunder. They occur simultaneously, but sound, traveling slower than light, reaches you later, creating the illusion of two separate events." — Goodreads 592471; quotepark 1356149 (Ch.3); azquotes 1119868; quotefancy 923764; Wikisource shows it near a "Chapter IV" heading (conflict). Top quote on Goodreads 52202.
   https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/592471-thought-can-never-capture-the-movement-of-life-it-is

25. VERBATIM: "It (thinking) does not put an end to sorrow." (Goodreads version omits the parenthesis.) — Goodreads 52202; Wikisource Mind_is_a_Myth/I; WELL chapter1.html.
   https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Mind_is_a_Myth/I ; https://people.well.com/user/jct/chapter1.html

26. VERBATIM: "Your thinking endlessly about being free from sorrow is only more material for sorrow." — same passage (Mind is a Myth Ch.1).

27. VERBATIM: "Sorrow is there for you as long as you think. There is actually no sorrow there to be free from." — same passage.

28. VERBATIM: "Only if you reject all the other paths can you discover your own path." — Goodreads 729309 (Mind Is a Myth). inner-quest.org has a variant "respect all the other paths" (likely error). https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/729309-only-if-you-reject-all-the-other-paths-can-you

29. VERBATIM: "If you have the courage to touch life for the first time, you will never know what hit you. Everything man has thought, felt and experienced is gone, and nothing is put in its place." — Ch.4 "There Is Nothing To Understand"; Wikiquote; Goodreads 584727; quotepark 1836321; quotefancy 923773. (praveennv blog misprints "taught" for "thought".)
   https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/584727-if-you-have-the-courage-to-touch-life-for-the

30. VERBATIM fragment: "It is the ones who believe in God, who preach peace and talk of love, who have created the human jungle. Compared to ma[n]…" ; continuation [paraphrase]: the so-called "civilized" man kills for ideals and beliefs, while animals kill only for survival. — Ch.1; libquotes lbs1h8v; quotepark 1899973. https://libquotes.com/u-g-krishnamurti/quote/lbs1h8v

31. VERBATIM: "There is no such thing as truth. The only thing that is actually there is your "logically" ascertained premise, which you call "truth."" — Ch.1; quotepark 1787020; quotefancy 923781. https://quotepark.com/quotes/1787020-ug-krishnamurti-there-is-no-such-thing-as-truth-the-only-thing-th/

32. [paraphrase, near-verbatim fragments]: there has been "no qualitative change in man's thinking"; the only change is "our ability to destroy our neighbor and his property." — Ch.1; quotepark.

33. VERBATIM: "There is no such thing as spiritual or psychological enlightenment because there is no such thing as spirit or psyche [at all]." Followed by: "…it is the search itself which has been choking me and keeping me out of my natural state." — Wikiquote (Mind is a Myth 1987); remembering-ug "Passages from Mind is a Myth". Some reprints add "at all".
   http://remembering-ug.blogspot.com/2012/05/passages-220712.html

34. VERBATIM: "God or Enlightenment is the ultimate pleasure, uninterrupted happiness. No such thing exists." — Goodreads author quotes p.3 (listed under Mind Is a Myth / No Way Out); Wikisource Thought_is_Your_Enemy also has it (see conflicts). https://www.goodreads.com/author/quotes/30268.U_G_Krishnamurti?page=3

35. VERBATIM fragment: "I am not anti-rational, just unrational. You may infer a rational meaning in what I say or do, but it is your doing,…" — quotefancy 923758 (book not confirmed by search; usually Mind is a Myth Ch.1). https://quotefancy.com/quote/923758/...

36. VERBATIM fragment: "Nature is busy creating absolutely unique individuals, whereas culture has invented a single mold to which all must [conform]…" ; ends "…It is grotesque." [paraphrase-confirmed word]. — quotefancy 923745; quotlr. Book not confirmed by search (commonly Mind is a Myth).

37. VERBATIM fragment: "Food, clothing and shelter — these are the basic needs. Beyond that, if you…" — quotefancy 923757.

38. "damn fool" quote — [paraphrase only]; quotepark labels it Ch.4 but notes "Quoted in Introduction by Terry Newland" (so it is Newland quoting UG in the intro).

39. [paraphrase] (kwize, Mind is a Myth): both inner and outer worlds proceed from a point of thought; everything experienced is born out of thought, so experience is an illusion. https://kwize.com/en/authors/U-G-Krishnamurti/

40. [paraphrase] (kwize): tradition is a burden people cling to; it has to fall away on its own.

41. [paraphrase] (Goodreads author page): nobody knows anything about life.

42. [paraphrase] (quotepark/libquotes): society is "the human jungle"; it would be much easier to survive in nature's jungle.

## C. THOUGHT IS YOUR ENEMY (1990, ed. Frank Noronha & J.S.R.L. Narayana Moorty; Sowmya Publications, Bangalore, 1991; talks 1985-1990 in India, Switzerland, Australia, Netherlands, U.K.)

Chapter titles recovered: Ch.II "Throw Away Your Crutches"; Ch.III "The Robot Is Dreaming".

43. VERBATIM: "Don't follow me, I'm lost." — Goodreads 6212994 (7 quotes listed); Goodreads author page. https://www.goodreads.com/work/quotes/6212994-thought-is-your-enemy-mind-shattering-conversations-with-the-man-called

44. VERBATIM: "The demand to be prepared for all future actions and all situations is the cause of our problems. Every situation is so different…" [fragment]. Wikisource text reads "actions and situations" (quote sites: "actions and all situations"). Context [paraphrase]: we don't know what we would do in a given situation. — Goodreads 6212994; Wikisource; quotefancy; nichequotes.
   https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Thought_is_Your_Enemy

45. VERBATIM: "Society or culture or whatever you might want to call it, has created us all solely and wholly for the purpose of maintaining its continuity and status quo." — Wikiquote; libquotes lbt8d9s (Ch.II Throw Away Your Crutches); azquotes culture tag (Ch.III The Robot Is Dreaming) — conflict. https://libquotes.com/u-g-krishnamurti/quote/lbt8d9s

46. VERBATIM: "Thought has interfered and trying to suppress, control, and moralize about desire, to the detriment of mankind." Context [paraphrase]: desire is a bodily, natural matter. — Goodreads 6212994.

47. [paraphrase] (Goodreads 6212994): our experience of our own body depends on thought, and thought is memory.

48. [paraphrase] (Goodreads 6212994 / holybooks summary): there is no such thing as your mind or my mind; a separate individual mind is an illusion; thought is a protective mechanism.

49. VERBATIM fragments (Wikisource TIYE / No Way Out ch.1): "transformation, moksha, and all that stuff are just variations of the same theme: permanent happiness" ; "the body cannot sustain that kind of pleasure" [paraphrase]. https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Thought_is_Your_Enemy ; https://people.well.com/user/jct/enemy0.htm

50. [paraphrase] remembering-ug "Passages from Thought is Your Enemy": the fixed "I" as a reference point; the courage needed to drop one's conditioning; rejecting what others offer is the real task. http://remembering-ug.blogspot.com/2012/09/passages-from-thought-is-your-enemy.html (also /2012/10/…)

51. VERBATIM fragment (libquotes, book not given; commonly TIYE): "…everything you stand for, believe in, experience and aspire to is the result of thought. And thought is destructive because it is nothing more than a protective mechanism." quotefancy variant: thought is "nothing more than a protective mechanism, programmed to protect its own interests at all costs". https://libquotes.com/u-g-krishnamurti/quotes/thought

52. VERBATIM fragment (libquotes peace page, book not given): "everything you are doing to be at peace with yourself is what is destroying the peace that is already there." https://libquotes.com/u-g-krishnamurti/quotes/peace

53. VERBATIM fragment (quotefancy, book not given): "…the constant use of thought to give continuity to your separate self."

54. VERBATIM fragment (quotefancy, book not given): "the fear of losing what we have and what we know" is the cause of our problems.

## Attribution conflicts
- "To realize that there is no enlightenment at all is enlightenment": Wikisource = Part Four; kwize = Part Three.
- "Unquestioned action is morality" / "What is morality?": most sites = Mystique; one Goodreads listing = Mind Is a Myth.
- "Thought can never capture the movement of life": quotepark/azquotes = Mind is a Myth Ch.3 "Not Knowing Is Your Natural State"; Wikisource places it under a "Chapter IV" heading.
- "God or Enlightenment is the ultimate pleasure…": Goodreads = Mind Is a Myth and No Way Out; Wikisource has it in Thought is Your Enemy.
- "Society or culture … continuity and status quo": libquotes = TIYE Ch.II "Throw Away Your Crutches"; azquotes = TIYE Ch.III "The Robot Is Dreaming".
- "The search ends with the realization that there is no such thing as enlightenment": azquotes = Mystique; Wikiquote places the related "search … choking me" passage under Mind is a Myth.
- "To be yourself requires extraordinary intelligence": Goodreads/Wikiquote = Mystique; some aggregators = No Way Out; inner-quest shows it as a talk transcript.
- "The demand to be prepared…": wording differs — Wikisource "all future actions and situations" vs quote sites "all future actions and all situations".
- "damn fool": quotepark labels Mind is a Myth Ch.4 but says quoted in Terry Newland's Introduction.
- "reject all the other paths" vs inner-quest "respect all the other paths".
- Wikiquote notes Goodreads community quotes are unverified; several Goodreads TIYE-page quotes are actually tagged Mind Is a Myth or No Way Out.
