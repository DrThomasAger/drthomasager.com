# Bernadette Roberts (1931–2017) — Images catalogue

Scope: photographs, book covers (all editions and translations found), documents, video stills and place photographs relating to Bernadette Roberts, the American Catholic contemplative, author of *The Experience of No-Self*. Other people named Bernadette Roberts (the Syracuse NY, Reading PA, Clinton IA obituaries; Bernadette Soubirous files on Commons) are excluded.

Method: ~30 web searches (extended mode), October 2026. No page fetches were possible, so entries marked **lead** record where an image is reported or expected but not visually confirmed. Machine-readable version: `images.json` (24 records).

Summary: 24 records — 11 book covers, 7 photographs, 4 video stills, 2 documents; 16 confirmed (S), 8 leads. No freely licensed (Commons / CC) portrait of her exists.

## 5. Places
- **Carmel of Saint Teresa, 215 E Alhambra Rd, Alhambra CA** — the Discalced Carmelite monastery she entered in January 1949 aged 17 (stayed c. 8.5–9 years). Photos: Around Alhambra, Dec 2022, "Only in Alhambra: The Cloistered Discalced Carmelite Nuns of the Monastery of Saint Teresa" https://issuu.com/aroundalhambra/docs/aa_dec_1222_web/s/17478054 ; https://cloisteredlife.com/directory/alhambra ; monastery site carmelteresa.org. No Commons file found.
- **New Camaldoli Hermitage, Big Sur** — annual extended retreats for the last forty years of her life. Freely licensed photos via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Camaldoli_Hermitage (Commons); Flickr, Heather Polley, 1 Jan 2020 https://www.flickr.com/photos/hpolley/49794781251 (licence to check); Angelus News feature https://angelusnews.com/voices/new-camaldoli-hermitage/.
- **Home, Laguna Beach CA** (died there 27 Nov 2017, ALS) — no photo found.
