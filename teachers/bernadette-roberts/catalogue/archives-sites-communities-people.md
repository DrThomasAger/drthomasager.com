# Bernadette Roberts (1931 – 27 Nov 2017): archives, sites, communities, people

Scope: the American Catholic contemplative, author of *The Experience of No-Self*. Other people named Bernadette Roberts (obituaries in NY, OH, PA, FL; Bernadette Tynan etc.) were excluded. Compiled 2026-10-25 from 28 web searches; nothing was fetched directly, so "status" means "appeared in search results", not "verified live". Machine-readable versions: `scratchpad/json-br/archives.json` (34 rows) and `scratchpad/json-br/people.json` (24 rows).

## 1. Sites run by her or for her

| Site | Who runs it | What it holds | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **bernadetteroberts.blogspot.com** | set up c. 2013 in her own first-person voice; maintained after 2017 by her friends | Books page (full essay table of contents), "Recorded Talks" page selling the DVD set *The Essence of Christian Mysticism* (nine hours, four files, handouts) and the DVD *A Passage Through Self*; front-page death announcement (died in her sleep 27 Nov 2017 at home in Southern California, ALS diagnosed a year earlier, "she asked us to notify her friends") | online |

## 6. People

**Family** — Ron Danko (former husband, left 1976); children Mark, Marcel, Melanie, Michaela "Kayla" Danko; seven grandchildren (all per Wikipedia). No child is publicly named as executor.

**Students / custodians** — the anonymous "Bernadette's Friends" circle (Blogspot sites, Fullerton retreats, DVD sales, death notice, ContemplativeChristians.com imprint) — de facto holders of her literary estate and recordings. Dan Garmat and Heidi Munn (TAT, 2006 retreat).

**Interviewers / editors / publishers** — Stephan Bodian (1986 interview); Jerry Katz (nonduality.com; *One*, 2007); Connie Shaw (Sentient); SUNY Press; Shambhala; Shawn Ellison (YouTube, secondary).

**Scholars who treat her** — Jim Marion (*Putting on the Mind of Christ*, 2000, foreword Ken Wilber); Ken Wilber (foreword; listed with her as nondual writer; direct citations unconfirmed); Philip St. Romain; James Arraj; Nini Praetorius (Copenhagen, commissioned "What Is Self?" paper); Douglas Lockhart; Carl McColman. Bernard McGinn and Jeffrey Kripal: no connection found.

**Monastic friends** — Camaldolese monks of New Camaldoli Hermitage, Big Sur (forty years of retreats).
