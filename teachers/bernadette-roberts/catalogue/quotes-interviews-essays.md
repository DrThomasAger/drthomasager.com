# Bernadette Roberts (1931 – 27 Nov 2017): quotations from interviews, essays and letters

Scope: the Catholic contemplative and author of *The Experience of No-Self*. Other people named Bernadette Roberts are excluded.
Method: web search only (snippets of reposted texts); no page was fetched in full. Every item below is therefore
confidence **T** (tentative) until checked against the printed source. "Verbatim" means the snippet reproduced
the words directly; "fragment" means part of a sentence; "paraphrase" means someone else's rendering of her words.
Machine-readable copy: `scratchpad/json-br/quotes-b.json` (18 entries).

## 1. Interview with Stephan Bodian — "The Experience of No-Self", *Yoga Journal*, Nov/Dec 1986
Reprinted in Stephan Bodian (ed.), *Timeless Visions, Healing Voices* (1991). Reposted at realization.org,
spiritualteachers.org, awakeningtoreality.com, o-meditation.com ("From the Unitive State to No-Self"),
standinginanopenfield.wordpress.com, rob-being.blogspot.com, taooflightyoga.com, thedaobums.com.
The interview was conducted in writing at her request (see Bodian's introduction, §4 below).

- "Initially, I gave up looking for this experience in the Buddhist literature." — verbatim
- "I met up with Buddhism only at the end of my journey, after the no-self experience." — verbatim
- "Four years later, however, I came across two lines attributed to Buddha describing his enlightenment experience.
  Referring to self as a house, he said, 'All thy rafters are broken now, the ridgepole is destroyed.'" — fragment
  (first clause reconstructed from a third-person summary; the Buddha line is as reposted). She adds that without
  the ridgepole there can be no house, no self.
- "the divine center – the coin, or 'true self' – suddenly disappeared, and without center or circumference there is
  no self, and no divine." — fragment (repeated identically in several summaries)
- "some 25 years after the transforming process" — fragment (dating of the no-self event; another repost says
  "some 20 years after leaving the convent")
- "I don't think we should get locked into any stage theory" — fragment, as quoted by a later commentator

No Katz–Roberts interview or Q&A was found. Roberts appears in Katz's anthology *One: Essential Writings on

## 3. Essays
Collected in *Essays on the Christian Contemplative Journey* (2007, 161 pp.) and *The Christian Contemplative
Journey: Essays on the Path* (ContemplativeChristians.com Publishing, Dec 2017, ISBN 978-0692987216). Titles listed
on the friends' blog (bernadetteroberts.blogspot.com): "What is Self? – a research paper", "Image and Likeness",
"The Eucharist, A Christian Path", "Christ as the Definition of 'Non-duality'", "The Ox Herding Pictures",
"The Nature of Self"; the 2007 TOC also covers nonduality, mystical theology and no-self, apatheia, means–end,
an interview, and women and mysticism. No essay titled "The Christian Passage" was found; *What Is Self?* has a
chapter "Steps in My Christian Passage".

- "Christ is the eternal oneness of two natures, divine and human." — verbatim, opening of *Christ as the Definition
  of Non-duality* (blog repost)
- "Unfortunately, what most Buddhist authors define as the no-self experience is actually the no-ego experience."
  — verbatim, *What Is Self?* (Shalom Place forum cites pp. 49–53, 115)
- "the terms 'self' and 'consciousness' express the same experiences, and so no-self, then, means
  no-consciousness" — fragment; attributed by a summary to "her interview" (probably the interview section of
  *What Is Self?*)
- "without a personal God, the Buddhist must have a much stronger faith in the 'unconditioned and unbegotten'";
  the Christian passage is "a divine doing, and in no way a self-doing" — fragments / possible paraphrase of her
  Buddhist–Christian comparison (Dao Bums thread; innerexplorations.com)
- Response to Ken Wilber: none found. Wilber is reported (josephbray.wordpress.com) to push back on her
  "unitive" ceiling; no Roberts essay replying to Wilber surfaced.

## 4. Statements on misrepresentation (reported, not her words)
Stephan Bodian, introduction to the 1986 interview: "Roberts was reluctant at first, protesting that others who
had tried had distorted her meaning... Instead of a live interview, she suggested, why not send her a list of
questions to which she would respond in writing". (Ellipsis in the snippet.) spiritualteachers.org adds that
written answers "would eliminate the chance of misunderstanding".

## 5. Letters
- To Philip St. Romain (Shalom Place), extensive correspondence; he recalls her writing that "the contemplative
  must journey alone" / "this way must be traveled alone" — paraphrase (his recollection).
- St. Romain also reports having "quibbled with Bernadette Roberts many times over her, 'God is everything that
  exists,' with the qualifier 'except the self.'" — fragments.
- Richard (actualfreedom.com.au) "Selected Correspondence: Ms. Bernadette Roberts" is correspondence *about* her,
  not by her; it cites a 1982 piece "Pure Subjectivity". Not a Roberts source.

## 6. Book passages that surfaced (for cross-reference; not interview material)
- "It never goes outside itself to know itself because the unmanifested, the manifesting, and the manifested are
  One." — "How it Works", *The Experience of No-Self* (read aloud in the spiritualteachers.org podcast, 16 May 2021)
- "and when I finally saw 'that' which remains when there is no self, I thought of Christ and how he too had seen
  'that' which remained - a seeing which is the resurrection itself." — quoted by James Arraj
- "the second movement as a continuation and completion of the first" — fragment quoted by Arraj

## 7. Obituary / memorial material
- Wikipedia and westernmystics.wordpress.com: she "often said" that "Camaldoli is my only true home on this earth"
  (no primary source). She made annual extended retreats with the Camaldolese monks at Big Sur for about forty years
  and for thirty years gave an annual retreat "The Essence of Christian Mysticism" (Trinity, Christ, Faith).
- Friends' announcement (bernadetteroberts.blogspot.com, via search summary): she died in her sleep on
  27 November 2017, having been diagnosed with ALS a year earlier; "she is (as she always was) with God";
  "we will miss her dearly and are grateful for all she gave us". These are the friends' words, not hers.
- No *What Is Enlightenment?* or *Tricycle* interview was found.
