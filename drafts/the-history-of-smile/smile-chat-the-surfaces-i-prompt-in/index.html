Smile Chat, the Surfaces I Prompts In

Smile Chat is where I prompt. It began in July 2026 as a chat window beside a local model, and from September I asked for the RuneLite chat to become a full chat too.

Dr Thomas Ager18 October 20264 min read

(claude wrote this for me)

THE MAP names Smile Chat as where the token lens lives, so any alternative token it shows can be taken, where Smile games are played live, and as one of the places the living linter reaches. Smile Chat has no heading of its own in THE MAP, and these are the words of mine that built it.

It began on 22 July 2026, separated from the command line. “can you separate the window?”, “replace it with "(: Smile Chat"”, “I want ctrl+N to open a new chat.”, and the reason to begin small, “we can build as a companion first and integrate features into OpenWebUI after.”1 On 23 July it took the controls of careful prompting, a button that “deletes what the model has just said”, and “i cant tell the difference between spaces and tabs visually. could you make it so?”2 On 25 July, “Smile Chat is sufficient, not Smile Chat Room.”3

Smile Chat

Begun 25 July 2026, 17 commits, README at C:/Users/hanjo/Claude/Projects/Smile/Smile Chat/README.md.

Smile Chat is the chat app of Smile, a local model on my own PC spoken to through an interface I own, where every exchange is kept as a receipt. It is the engine meant to drive smileprompt.ai.
