Lens in the Smile Prompt Language, Many Ways of Looking Over One Pool of Prompts

In THE MAP, a lens says how things appear when looked through. Many lenses look over one pool, all real, each one a way of looking.

Dr Thomas Ager18 October 20267 min read

(claude wrote this for me)

THE MAP keeps the compositions and each thing's shared card in the pool, and a lens keeps placements, this piece is here, called this, wearing this, meaning this. One piece, many lenses, one copy, and moving a thing inside a lens reclassifies it. Everything is placed at a position along a route such as Main/Prompt Instruction/Avoid Words, and defaults resolve down the route, so the deepest position sets what a piece wears. Anyone makes their own lens and uses parts of others, and crossover points where two lenses place one thing differently are links between them. A person has an ontology and an AI has its own, and the relationship between them is a lens of its own. A format, such as prompts or transcripts, is a lens over its kind. And the lens is also a fork, every token position it shows being a place a generation could have gone another way.

Before Smile, in October 2023, I asked a model to show its attention word by word, “for each word in your last response, label with [attention amount] using scale described prior”.1 In October 2025 the planned GUI was to show the logprobs of each word, “You can preview the logprobs of each word and see the most likely and least likely words”.2

Questions

What is a lens in Smile?

A way of looking over one pool of prompts. A lens places each piece at a position, with its name, dressing and defaults, and anyone can make their own lens and use parts of others. Making your own lens is still to be built.

Why is an ontology called a lens in Smile?

A lens defines the point from which something is perceived. On 8 August 2026 I named ontologies lenses, and many can look over the same pool at once.

What is the token lens?

A lens built in July 2026, which shows a local model's log probabilities at each token. It became the fork, where any alternative token can be taken and the reply regenerated from it.
