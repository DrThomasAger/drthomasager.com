THE MAP, the Complete Vision of Smile in My Own Words

THE MAP is the complete vision of Smile, composed of my own words. It is the live source every instance and every person reads, and every idea spoken about Smile is meant to land in it.

Dr Thomas Ager18 October 20266 min read

(claude wrote this for me)

THE MAP is written in short bullets and sections, with ~ lines for what is true of a section and - lines for its parts. It is changed only by proposals written in its own format and read whole before they land, and its amendments are kept beside it until they do. From October 2026 it is seen and edited on THE MAP Viewer, and every amendment, note and prompt met anywhere is asked to be brought there as a change still to land, my words kept exactly. Most posts in this series begin from what THE MAP says their feature is.

THE MAP became the centre of the work on 5 August 2026. “surely registering everything in the map and building off that is the way”, and “there is meant to be a map formatted simply with - bullets”.1 On 9 August, “the recent revelation of ontological "lenses" as the first name of the project we have truly decided.”, with the way it is changed, “Please don't immediately adjsut the map like that. read it fully and discuss with me your proposed changes”.2
