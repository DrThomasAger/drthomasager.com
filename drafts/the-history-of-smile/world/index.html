World in Smile, a Game World Agents See and Act In, Read Tick by Tick

In THE MAP, a world is a simulated game world that agents see and act in. The server's tick log is the raw stream, and the world at any tick is a working read over that log.

Dr Thomas Ager18 October 20265 min read

(claude wrote this for me)

THE MAP joins every exchange in the world to the same stream, each Gana's words with the model's parameters and timing. A frame is named by its hash and stamped with the tick it shows, and what a vision model said of it is scored claim by claim against the world at the same tick, so a difference between what was drawn and what was true is a finding. The in-game Gana interface is the working read for one reader at one moment, the exact next step and everything the person lacks for that decision. Sangha play against Sangha in formations the way pieces are placed in chess, and the entities of a world never team together against the humans in it.

Questions

What is a world in Smile?

A simulated game world agents see and act in, such as Vijaya Ganapati's Realm. Its tick log is the raw stream, and the world at any tick is read from it.

What does the in-game Gana interface show?

Only the exact next step and what the person lacks for that decision, computed when the state changes and never stored.
