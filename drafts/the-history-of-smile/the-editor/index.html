The Smile Editor, From the Smile Composer GUI to an Editor That Knows the Faces

In THE MAP, the editor reads the version and the defaults in use, such as the five-tier hierarchy, four-space indentation and the header as a face, a label and a face. Changing one face updates its matched faces, so the four faces of a section change in one edit.

Dr Thomas Ager18 October 20267 min read

(claude wrote this for me)

THE MAP lets an edit reach below the face, to the mouth or the nose alone. The part is its own piece, so changing it recomposes every face gathering it and their paired closers with them. The reach of a part edit is set as everywhere else in Smile, changed at the part's tier for every face, or kept at the face's tier as an override of that one face. Writing at any tier is assisted by what the version binds, such as the dressing for the depth being written at.

Questions

What is the Smile editor?

In THE MAP, an editor that reads the Smile version and defaults in use, so changing one face updates its matched faces, and editing a single part such as a mouth recomposes every face that gathers it. Changing one face with its pairs is part built.

What was the Smile Composer?

The Smile GUI built from October 2025, a keyboard-driven tree of agents, modules and instructions that became a two panel editor with a hierarchy tree and a text editor.
