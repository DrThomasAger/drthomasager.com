THE MAP names this purpose through the four immeasurables. Mettā, every syntax smiles. Karuṇā, phrases that harm are replaced with phrases that ask for attention compassionately. Muditā, every part enjoys the others and grows with them. Upekkhā, anyone can make their own lens and use parts of ours.
