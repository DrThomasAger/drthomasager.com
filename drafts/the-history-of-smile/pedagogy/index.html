Pedagogy in Smile, Teaching Made for One Person From Their Own Prompts

In THE MAP, the goal is fully interpretable, automatically individuated and improved onto-epistemological pedagogy. A lesson is made for one learner from their own material, such as a section of their own prompt, and teaches why it works.

Dr Thomas Ager18 October 20263 min read

(claude wrote this for me)

THE MAP names each word of that goal. Pedagogy is what the Prompt Doctor and Smile make, episodes, posts, prompts and games. Onto-epistemological, it teaches what something is and how it is known, together. Individuated, each person receives the teaching made for them. Automatic, the making happens without a hand each time. Improved, each version is better than the one before. Fully interpretable, every choice in it can be traced. The learner's question after a lesson is the material of the next, so every lesson is co-created, and a lesson is a composition that sends.

In October 2023 I sketched a prompt scored while it is typed, a seed of teaching. “Here's an idea: We derive as many metrics as we can so that live, while the user is typing, we have a model that is predicting the assistants response”, scoring it as the prompt is written,1 and, of what would help most, “i want to understand better how prompts and different parts of prompt lead to response”.2
