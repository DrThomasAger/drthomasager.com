Section in the Smile Prompt Language, a Thing Wearing a Header and a Footer

In THE MAP, a section is the term for a thing wearing a header and footer wrapper, such as a Smile Section. Markdown gives a header alone, XML a header and a matching footer, and Smile makes both optional in any combination.

Dr Thomas Ager18 October 20267 min read

(claude wrote this for me)

THE MAP sets Smile's wrappers by the setting in use, and the default carries headers and footers of different hierarchy tiers, so the depth of a section is visible in its faces. Underneath, a section is a position in a Composition, and THE MAP keeps its parts in Module, the prefix, start face, label, end face and postfix of each line, and a body with a header, body and footer of its own.

Before Smile, in October 2023, I wrote of sections as something prompting already did. On 3 October, a prompt can be illegible “as long as the top and bottom are clearly described as to what it is doing.”1 On 31 October, “GPT-4 has a limited amt it can do per response, unless you semantically separate out different things into roles/rounds/sections”, and “These are both semantic separators.”2

The words its header carries are the Label, and the response sections are followed in Semantic Markdown.

Clearer separations, and top delimiters called section headers because they have smiley heads, is the section header named.

Not only do we separate concerns and allow different tasks to be composed together (eg identifying individual mistakes to rewrite and then using those to rewrite, or identifying the order or shape of the output first as a template/structure, then filling it in), we also give opportunity for granularly giving the model more aid in following the instructions.
Now when the model goes to fill out these sections, we have all of our corrections and suggestions for completing the task well inside of the area it needs to read to follow them. This lets us iterate on section behavior in the prompt modularly. It also lets us add specific text scaffolding to sections so that we can steer even more precisely.
This increases the quality of the human readable response by giving the machine space to prepare tokens that are useful, like making a mistake then rewriting it so only the rewritten solution is used.
There may also be specific problems like the mistakes being identified not actually being mistakes. These are some reasons why we want to make our response format, our output sections, explicit:

Questions

What is a section in Smile?

The term for a thing wearing a header and footer wrapper. In Smile both are optional in any combination, and the default gives headers and footers of different hierarchy tiers.

How do Smile sections differ from Markdown and XML?

Markdown gives a section a header alone, and XML a header and a matching footer. Smile lets either be present, and its faces show how deep the section sits.

What is the difference between a section and a module?

In August 2026 I said a module is a section. Both are names a lens gives to a position in a composition, with header, body and footer.
