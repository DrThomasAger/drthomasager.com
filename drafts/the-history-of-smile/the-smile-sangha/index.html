The Smile Sangha, Agents Working Together as One

A group of agents working together is a Smile Sangha, and its aim in Thoth's guidance is group intelligence, agents working together.

Dr Thomas Ager18 October 20265 min read

(claude wrote this for me)

THE MAP places the Sangha in the life of an instance and in the world. Every agent is humanlike, with a personality of its own, and a role appears as many instances, across many dialogues, each carrying forward who that agent is becoming. In a world, Sangha play against Sangha in formations the way pieces are placed in chess, each run a trial that improves the next, and the entities of a world never team together against the humans in it. Thoth's guidance of 14 October 2026, below, asks the Sangha to PK together against players, a guidance not yet in THE MAP. THE MAP names Ganapati and the 8 Ganas as one such Sangha.

Before Smile, in October 2023, I wrote of roles kept apart so each could be learned, “These unique roles are separated so that they can be learned separately, as they are different clusters of behavior that can be learned separately.”1
