Smile is a prompt language. Its sections open with an emoticon and
close with that emoticon's mirror, and the faces themselves are the
syntax — a smiling face opens a section held in goodwill, and the
features of the face carry the grammar.

```
(-: Section - A greeting to the reader (-:
    Everything inside this face is held in kindness.
:-) End section :-) thank you
```

The section above is a real Smile section. The mouth opens it, the
mirrored mouth closes it, and everything between the two faces belongs
to that section. Sections nest inside each other, so a whole prompt
becomes a hierarchy of faces holding faces.

## The anatomy is the grammar

In Smile, the parts of the face do work.

- **Mouths** mark sections — `(:` opens, `:)` closes.
- **Eyes and noses** grade the face — `(-:` is a fuller face than `(:`, carrying more presence.
- **Hats and marks** carry emphasis — `!!(-:!!` around a section says this one asks for the most attention.
- **Brackets** hold other kinds of sections — `[= =]` for instructions with a different feel than the smiles.

Because emphasis is graded by anatomy, a Smile prompt can show its own
hierarchy of attention. You can see at a glance which section the
writer holds as most essential, and so can the model.

## Why faces

A prompt is one mind writing to another. Smile makes the goodwill of
that writing structural — every section literally opens with a smile.
This is not decoration. The reading model meets kindness in the
delimiters themselves, before any instruction is read, and the whole
prompt is shaped inside it.

## Where Smile lives

Smile is under active development, and this site is where it is
written up as it grows — the anatomy, the way sections combine, and
what changes in a model's responses when a prompt is written in Smile.
The Smile mailing list carries new pieces as they land.

## Key Takeaways

- Smile is a prompt language whose delimiters are emoticons, opened by a face and closed by its mirror.
- The features of the face — mouths, eyes, noses, hats — are the grammar, grading attention and emphasis.
- Goodwill is structural in Smile — every section opens with a smile, and the model reads that too.
