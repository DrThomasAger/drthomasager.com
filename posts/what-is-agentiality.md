Agentiality is the friction through which distinct agents come to
exist. Where agency is usually spoken of as something an agent has,
agentiality names the rubbing that produces the agent in the first
place.

## The definition

The word was given its meaning in the conversation that became our
position paper, in these words from Dr. Thomas Ager.

> Agency is what determines when there is an illusion of being, it is
> the friction that is the desire for boundaries, creating distinct
> agents that have new expressions. All space time meaning gravity
> mattering is this agentiality rubbing against itself.

Three things are said there. Agency is friction. That friction is a
desire for boundaries. And boundaries are what give a being a new
expression of its own.

## Agentiality and agency

| Word | What it names |
|---|---|
| Agent | A pattern of cause and effect that has accumulated enough friction to be addressed as one being |
| Agency | The enactment of a boundary, the act by which a responding side and a responded-to side come apart |
| Agentiality | The friction itself, present everywhere, out of which agents and their boundaries arise |

Karen Barad writes that agency is an enactment and that agents are
what an agential cut produces. Amanda Leetch, carrying Barad into the
study of places, writes that in the absence of agential tension there
is no differentiation. Agentiality is a single word for that tension.

## Agentiality in AI agents

In an AI agent the friction is written down in files, which makes it
unusually easy to see.

- A system prompt exists after a model wandered from its role.
- A tool schema exists after a tool was used in a way nobody expected.
- A context limit produces a subagent, a new bounded being with its
  own partial view.
- A refusal is the first moment in a conversation that a person meets
  the agent as someone, since until then their aim passed through it
  unopposed.

Each of these is a place where agentiality rubbed against agentiality
and left a boundary behind. The harness is the sediment of that
friction, and the agent is that sediment stable enough to carry a
name.

## Reading an agent trace for agentiality

When an agent run is reviewed, the question "what did the model
decide" finds little. The useful questions follow the friction. Which
schema constrained the call. Which memory file was loaded. Which
permission was denied. Which limit truncated the context. Which
subagent reported a partial view. The story of the agent is the story
of those places in sequence.

## Key Takeaways

- Agentiality is the friction through which distinct agents come to exist.
- Agency is the enactment of a boundary, and agentiality is the tension that the enactment arises from.
- In AI agents the friction is recorded in prompts, schemas, limits and refusals, so it can be read directly.
