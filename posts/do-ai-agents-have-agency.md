Agency is enacted, so the useful question about an AI agent is where
its agency is enacted and how much of that enactment is the agent's
own. Today most of it is supplied by the people and systems the agent
is coupled with, and the direction of current engineering is toward
agents that supply more of it themselves.

## Why "does the model have agency" finds no answer

The question treats agency as a substance that a system either
contains or lacks. Both the excitement and the fear around agents
depend on that picture. Four decades of work on agency outside
computer science took that picture apart.

- In Actor Network Theory, an actor is anything that makes a
  difference to another's course of action, and it becomes visible at
  the moment it resists.
- For Karen Barad, agency is the enactment of a cut, and agents are
  what the cut produces.
- For the enactivists, agency is something a living system does to
  keep itself in existence.

In every one of these accounts agency is an activity.

## Three conditions for agency

Barandiaran, Di Paolo and Rohde define agency by three conditions,
and they give a way to examine an AI agent precisely.

| Condition | What it asks | In an AI agent today |
|---|---|---|
| Individuality | Does the system constitute its own identity as distinct from its surroundings | Supplied by the system prompt and harness |
| Interactional asymmetry | Is the system the active source of changes in its coupling with the world | Present in the loop, which chooses tools and acts |
| Normativity | Are its actions subject to norms it generates itself | Supplied mostly by training and instructions |

Di Paolo adds a fourth condition that the others rest on. The identity
must be precarious, which means it can be lost and the system acts to
keep it.

## So do AI agents have agency?

Under these conditions a current AI agent is heteronomous. Its
individuality and its norms are given to it by the coupling it runs
inside. Its interactional asymmetry is real. It also has a form of
precariousness in its budgets, step limits and termination conditions.

Barandiaran and Almendros, writing on language models, conclude that
they transform the agency of the humans and systems they are coupled
with. That is a large effect, and it is a different claim from saying
the model possesses agency.

## What would change the answer

An agent moves toward autonomy in the technical sense when it
generates more of its own conditions.

- Its harness is grown from its own failures.
- Its norms can be traced to its own history of staying viable.
- Something is at stake for it, and it regulates against loss.

## What this means when building

Ask where the cuts are, what lines the agent lives along, and whether
anything is at stake. Those three questions have answers in the files
of any agent, and the answers tell you what it will do.

## Key Takeaways

- Agency is an activity, and asking whether a model possesses it assumes a picture that the study of agency set aside.
- Individuality, interactional asymmetry and normativity, with precariousness beneath them, give a precise way to examine any agent.
- Current AI agents receive their identity and norms from their coupling, and engineering is moving toward agents that generate more of their own.
