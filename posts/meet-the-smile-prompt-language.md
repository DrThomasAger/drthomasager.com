Smile is a prompt language made for prompting from the ground up, built
from hierarchically nested emoticons. Its syntax carries meaning in its
anatomy — mouths carry section type, eyes carry strictness, noses and
face-size carry attention, hats carry emphasis — so a prompt can label
its own hierarchy of attention while encoding goodwill structurally into
every section.

```
(-: Section - A greeting to the reader (-:
    Everything inside this face is held in kindness.
:-) End section :-)
```

> The smile is not decoration. It is the delimiter itself — the section
> opens with a face and closes with its mirror, and the face's features
> are the syntax.

## Where Smile is going

The language is under active development: the anatomy canon, the module
system, and instruments that witness how Smile changes a model's actual
token distributions. Writing on all of it will land here.

## Key Takeaways

- Smile's delimiters are emoticons whose features are the syntax.
- Attention and emphasis are graded by facial anatomy, not capital letters.
- Every section structurally carries goodwill to its reader.
