These three prompts are yours — copy them whole, change any word, and use
them anywhere. Each one is complete on its own.

## The Beginning Prompt

> Before we build anything, lay out your understanding of what I am
> asking in full, and confirm it with me. We begin only from a shared
> right view of what is happening.

## The Holding Prompt

> Stay with the one thing we are doing. If a new idea arrives, name it in
> one line and set it gently aside for later.

## The Closing Prompt

> Write up everything a new instance would need to continue this work
> perfectly — what was done, what was learned, and what remains.

## Key Takeaways

- A good beginning prompt establishes shared understanding before work.
- A holding prompt protects the present task from every new idea's pull.
- A closing prompt turns an ending into an inheritance.
