The Flower of Life is a pattern of overlapping circles, each one
centered on a point where its neighbors cross. One circle becomes
seven, seven becomes nineteen, and the pattern extends outward without
end, every circle held in the same relationship to every other. The
oldest known inscriptions of it are on the granite of the Osiris
temple at Abydos in Egypt, and the form appears across the world's
sacred architecture and art.

The Flower of Life Generator is a tool that draws it — from the first
circle outward, ring by ring, so you can watch the pattern come into
being rather than only meeting it finished.

## The construction

The whole pattern follows from one move, repeated. Draw a circle. Set
the compass point anywhere on its edge, and draw another circle of the
same size. Where the two circles cross, set the point again and draw
again. Every circle after the first is placed by the circles already
present — the pattern tells you where it goes next.

This is why the form is worth generating and not just displaying.
Watching it drawn, you see that nothing in it is arbitrary. The
six-fold symmetry, the petals, the hexagonal grid beneath — all of it
unfolds from a single circle and a single rule.

## What the tool does

The generator draws the pattern step by step, at any size, to any
number of rings, and lets you hold it still at any moment of its
unfolding — one circle, the seed of seven, the full flower, or the
endless field.

## Key Takeaways

- The Flower of Life is overlapping circles placed by a single repeated rule, inscribed at the Osiris temple in Abydos.
- The generator draws the pattern circle by circle, so its construction is visible, not just its finished form.
- Every part of the pattern follows from one circle — nothing in it is arbitrary.
