The open hand holds everything<br>
by holding nothing back.<br>
What arrives is welcomed,<br>
what leaves is blessed on its way.

The closed fist keeps one coin<br>
and loses the whole river.<br>
The open hand keeps the river<br>
by letting every coin pass through.

> Sing this where giving has become counting, and the counting will
> remember it was always giving.

## Key Takeaways

- Holding nothing back is how everything is held.
- What leaves, blessed, remains as blessing.
- The river is kept only by the open hand.
