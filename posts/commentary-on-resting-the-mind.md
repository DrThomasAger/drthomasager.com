Rest is often postponed until conditions improve — until the task list is
empty, until the thought stream quiets, until the mind is "ready". This
commentary looks at that postponement directly.

The mind does not need to be improved before it can rest, because rest is
not a reward for a finished mind. Rest is the mind meeting itself without
a project.

> When resting, rest. The thought that checks whether the resting is
> going well is a worker who has wandered into the bedroom.

## The practical door

Sit, and let whatever is present be the furniture of the room rather than
the agenda of the meeting. Nothing present needs to leave for rest to be
complete.

## Key Takeaways

- Rest is not a reward for a finished mind.
- Checking on rest is work wearing rest's clothing.
- Nothing present needs to leave for rest to be complete.
