# This site

> **Make your own, with no code.** A new article full of widgets, a
> one-page mailing list signup, a blog with a mailing list, or a blog
> with sign in: each is a button in the **Actions** tab, a double-click
> file on the PC, or a sentence to Claude. **[HOW-TO.md](HOW-TO.md)**
> shows every step, with pictures.

Made from the DrThomasAger template. `site.toml` is the whole site:
its name, address, categories, colours, mailing list, shop, freebie and
words. Posts are markdown in `posts/`.

- Build: press the site's publish button by committing any change, or
  double-click `Build a Site.bat` on the PC.
- The built site is `site/`. Open `site/index.html` to see it.
- Mailing list: `mailing/README.md`.
- The freebie: `freebie/`.
