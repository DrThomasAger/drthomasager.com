"Tutorial: Your First Structured Prompt"

TADr Thomas Ager|Updated 2026-08-18|7 min readTutorial

A structured prompt is grown, not assembled. This tutorial walks one real prompt from a single sentence to a full structure, adding one element at a time so you can feel what each addition does.

Step 1. Say the one thing

Start with the single sentence that would be enough if the reader were wise: what you want, plainly.

Step 2. Add the context that is true

Add only context you know to be true — who you are, what exists already, what the writing is for.

Step 3. Draw the sections

Give each kind of content its own labelled section, so the prompt's attention structure is visible at a glance.

Step 4. Read it as the reader

Read the whole prompt as if you had never seen it, and remove everything the reader does not need.

Key Takeaways

  • Begin from one plain sentence and grow outward.
  • Every section earns its place by carrying one kind of content.
  • The final read is always done through the reader's eyes.
