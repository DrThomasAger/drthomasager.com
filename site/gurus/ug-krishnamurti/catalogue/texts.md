**Scope note.** Uppaluri Gopala Krishnamurti only; Jiddu Krishnamurti results were discarded (several appeared — e.g. a Google Books record mis-attributing *Mind is a Myth* to "Jiddu Krishnamurti", id=MVhaeewbyRYC — and were dropped or noted as cataloguing errors).

**Method / coverage warning.** 22 WebSearch (extended) queries completed before the shared per-turn search budget (200 calls across all agents) was exhausted; 12 further planned queries were refused by the tool. WebFetch/curl were not attempted per instructions. Marking: **[S]** = taken from a returned search result/summary in this session; **[U]** = background knowledge, not verified here (all [U] items are repeated in §9 "Gaps and unverified leads"). ISBN/page/year conflicts between retailers are reported as found, not resolved.
