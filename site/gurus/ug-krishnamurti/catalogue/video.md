# Catalogue of Video Recordings of U.G. Krishnamurti (1918–2007)

**Scope note.** All entries below concern Uppaluri Gopala (U.G.) Krishnamurti only. Results about Jiddu Krishnamurti (KFT/KFA channels, kfoundation.org, kfa.org, jkrishnamurti.org, krishnamurtisubtitles.info, Dailymotion "jiddu-krishnamurti" clips, "Thinking Allowed"-unrelated JK items) were encountered repeatedly and discarded.

**Research-tool note.** Only WebSearch was usable; 28 extended searches were completed before the session-wide WebSearch budget (200 calls/turn shared by all agents) ran out. Two planned searches (remembering-ug.blogspot talks index; Swiss/German/Cologne TV) were refused for that reason, and ~10 further planned searches (Facebook/Instagram/Telegram pages, Rumble/Bitchute, Russian/Spanish/Italian subtitled versions, tape-format provenance, Larry Morris/Birla item details, "Natural State Old TV Documentary" clip, Terry Newland/Narayana Moorty videos) were never run. These are listed under "Gaps". The user can send a follow-up message to continue the search.

## 2. Documentaries and films

- **The Natural State** (2026) — dir. Matthew Dougherty (New York / Hudson Valley); exec. producers Jane Campion and Mahesh Bhatt; 99 min (Upstate Films) / "100-minute" (Variety India). Built from hundreds of hours of camcorder footage shot by **Julie Thayer** (1989 onward, Swiss Alps/Gstaad and elsewhere, 1980s–90s) plus her journals; Bhatt interviewed in Varanasi. World premiere Telluride Film Festival, 7 Sept 2026; also Woodstock Film Festival; project 15–18 years in the making (Dougherger found Thayer's footage online in 2008). URLs: https://deadline.com/2026/09/the-natural-states-interview-jane-campion-matt-dougherty-1237068829/ ; https://www.upstatefilms.org/the-natural-state ; https://thefilmstage.com/woodstock-review-the-natural-state-examines-the-contradictory-state-of-non-belief/ ; https://www.hammertonail.com/interviews/matthew-dougherty/ ; https://theoverlooknews.com/a-film-18-years-in-the-making-found-its-footing-in-a-hudson-valley-whatsapp-group/ ; https://varietyindia.com/the-natural-state-review-decoding-indias-fiercest-anti-guru-u-g-krishnamurti/ ; https://varietyindia.com/mahesh-bhatt-on-matthew-doughertys-the-natural-state-the-film-has-been-born-the-director-has-been-born-ug-krishnamurti-has-escaped/ ; https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/entertainment/ug-threw-me-back-into-life-mahesh-bhatt-on-the-brutal-beautiful-mystery-of-ug-krishnamurti ; https://www.newsbeep.com/au/881212/ ; earlier production report (Bhatt shooting for the documentary): https://www.bollywoodlife.com/ndtv/mahesh-bhatt-shoots-for-a-documentary-based-on-eminent-thinker-ug-krishnamurtis-life-194380/

URLs: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9HSURZ4Ca3I ; Part 1 "Something Real": https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rXi8xvjdqL4 ; Part 2 "Finished!": https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rNJ3oDLkoX0 ; Odysee: https://odysee.com/@librarybeyondbeyond:a/u-g-krishnamurti-finished-documentary:5

## 6. Original tapes, formats, digitisation, masters
- **Julie Thayer** — shot hundreds of hours on consumer camcorder from 1989 through the 1990s (Gstaad, Switzerland and elsewhere); footage described as grainy camcorder video; her journal mentions taking videotapes "to be transferred". Specific format (VHS-C/Hi8/MiniDV) not stated in any source found. Dougherty discovered the footage online c. 2008 and built *The Natural State* from it; the masters are presumably with Thayer/Dougherty's production — unverified.
- **S.C. Birla (Gstaad 1989)**, **Larry Morris (Albuquerque 1992)**, **Sydney/Amsterdam gatherings** — digitised as RealVideo for the WELL "Essential UG" site (late 1990s, by "jct") and later as archive.org items (~2012); original shooters/formats not recorded in sources.
- **TV items** (Doordarshan 1989, ASIA 1993, Kleurnet 2000, ABC 1990, Thinking Allowed 1987, Conscious TV 1989) — broadcaster masters presumed (Betacam/U-matic era) but no source confirms.
- Krishnamurti Foundation Trust's 2010 video digitisation project concerns J. Krishnamurti only (excluded).
