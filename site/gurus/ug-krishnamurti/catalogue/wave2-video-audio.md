# U.G. Krishnamurti — Wave 2 video & audio research

Second-wave web-search sweep (WebSearch only; no page fetches) on U.G. Krishnamurti (1918–2007) audio-visual holdings. Jiddu Krishnamurti material was excluded. 92 distinct items are catalogued in the companion JSON (`scratchpad/json/wave2-av.json`). Confidence: **S** = a search result directly described the item; **lead** = inferred, needs confirmation. ~44 searches used.

- Swiss TV, German/Cologne TV, RAI: **nothing found**.
- Indian TV confirmed: Doordarshan Rajiv Mehrotra; Ramola Bachchan 'Face to Face' 1993; Shashi Kumar; Mahesh Bhatt programme. UK: Conscious TV / Iain McNay 1989 (3 parts).

## Gap 6 — Tape provenance

- Julie Thayer: filmed UG continuously 1989–2007; 'hundreds of hours' of 'lo-fi home videos' (consumer camcorder; format not named in press); digitised by director Matt Dougherty for The Natural State (Telluride 2026-09-07; EPs Jane Campion, Mahesh Bhatt) https://www.hammertonail.com/interviews/matthew-dougherty/ , https://www.documentaries.org/films/the-ug-krishnamurti-documentary/.
- Chandrasekhar (Bangalore): files, photos, video and audio tapes moved to Yercaud at UG's insistence; Dr. Desiraju's life-story sessions recorded on tape; Chandrasekhar compiled dialogue excerpts from audio tapes https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Stopped_in_Our_Tracks:_Stories_of_U.G._in_India , https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Science_and_U.G.
- Henk Schonewille (Amsterdam, Sept 1982): c. 24 h of audio tapes, source of The Courage to Stand Alone (transcribed by Ellen Chrystal) https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Courage_to_Stand_Alone.
- David Bohm 1968 conversation: listed by the David Bohm Society as held at 'the Krishnamurti Archive' https://www.davidbohmsociety.org/david-bohm/audio-and-video/.
- thisrobotdreaming.com: author digitised all personal UG cassettes (1960s–90s) to MP3 and uploaded to archive.org https://www.thisrobotdreaming.com/blog/u-g-krishnamurti-tapes — **archive.org item ID not recovered** (behind a 'listen here' link).
- Mahesh Bhatt, Terry Newland, Noronha, Rodney Arms, Valentine de Kerven tapes: **nothing found**. No VHS/Hi8/Betacam/MiniDV format statements found anywhere.
