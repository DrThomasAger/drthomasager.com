# U.G. Krishnamurti (1918–2007): Catalogue of Archives, Websites, Communities and Physical Holdings

**Scope note.** Uppaluri Gopala Krishnamurti only. Every hit about Jiddu Krishnamurti, the Krishnamurti Foundations (KFA/KFT/KFI), Friedrich Grohe's "The Link" newsletter, kfoundation.org, kfa.org and the X account @K__Quotes was discarded.

**Method / limitation.** 27 WebSearch (extended) queries completed; the turn's shared search budget was then exhausted and 12 further queries (Travels with UG, ugarchive.blogspot, Mukunda Rao, Telegram/WhatsApp/Discord, Facebook groups, Narayana Moorty, Terry Newland/Rosestone/de Kerven, birthplace memorials, Italian/German/Dutch/Spanish/Russian sites, Telugu/Kannada sites, Smriti Books/e-artnow) were refused. No page could be fetched directly. Tags used below:
- **[V]** = confirmed by a search result in this session (URL seen in results).
- **[K]** = from background knowledge, not confirmed this session; verify before relying on it.
- **[?]** = named in the task brief but neither confirmed nor refuted by any result.

## 1. Websites devoted to U.G. (past and present)

### 1.1 ugkrishnamurti.org
- **Name:** ugkrishnamurti.org ("U.G. Krishnamurti" main site, c. 2000s).
- **Who ran it:** Not established by search. [K] Historically associated with the Bangalore "friends of UG" circle around K. Chandrasekhar / Poornakutee and the editors of the Indian books (Noronha/Moorty); it was the referent for "the UG site" during his lifetime.
- **Contents:** Full texts of the books (Wikipedia Talk page says both .org and .net carry "the books in their entirety") [V]; audio recordings — Internet Archive item `ug_dr_kbr_sastrii_bramchari_babu` is credited "by: http://ugkrishnamurti.org" [V]; a Wikipedia-derived page gives https://www.ugkrishnamurti.org as the official website [V].
- **Status:** Defunct/unresolved as a live site in this session; search engines returned no current page from the domain [V]. Captures exist in the Wayback Machine (not enumerated in this session) — check https://web.archive.org/web/*/ugkrishnamurti.org [K].
- **URLs:** http://ugkrishnamurti.org ; https://web.archive.org/web/*/ugkrishnamurti.org* ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talk:U._G._Krishnamurti/Archive_1 (discussion of the site) [V].

### 1.2 ugkrishnamurti.net
- **Who runs it:** Not established. Dharmapedia: "U.G. Krishnamurti.net, where most of the published works by, and about, U.G. can be found and read freely" [V]. It is the website linked from the X account @krishnamurtiug and from the Facebook page facebook.com/ugknowayout (which links to http://ugkrishnamurti.net/ugkrishnamurti-net/index.htm) [V]. Encyclopedia.com cites http://ugkrishnamurti.net/ as the UG site and reprints material from it [V]. SpiritualTeachers.org calls a site with audio, video and biography "the closest thing to an official U.G. Krishnamurti website" [V].
- **Contents:** Full texts of UG books and books about him; audio; video; biography; "No Way Out" branding (cf. FB handle ugknowayout) [V].
- **Status:** Domain referenced by live social accounts in 2023–24; current page content not retrieved this session. Treat as live-but-unverified.
- **URLs:** http://ugkrishnamurti.net/ ; http://ugkrishnamurti.net/ugkrishnamurti-net/index.htm.

## 6. People, publishers and holders of material
- **Mahesh Bhatt** — author *U.G. Krishnamurti: A Life* (Viking/Penguin India 1992; revised ed. adds "A Taste of Death", 30 days in Gstaad 1995; audiobook narrated by Bhatt, 11 h 46 min) [V]; conducted the cremation [V]; TV conversation on archive.org [V]. No institutional deposit of his tapes/papers found [V-negative].
- **Julie Thayer** — filmed UG's travels; *Get Lost and Stay Lost: On the Road with UG Krishnamurti*; introduction to Non-Duality Press *Mind Is a Myth* [V]; Travels with UG site [K].
- **Louis Brawley** — *Goner: The Final Travels of UG Krishnamurti* (Non-Duality Press, Salisbury UK, 2011; rev. 2016; ISBN 978-0-9566432-7-8, 394 pp.); photographer/artist; blog louisbrawley.wordpress.com [V].
- **Mukunda Rao** — *The Other Side of Belief: Interpreting U.G. Krishnamurti* (Penguin Books India 2005, ISBN 9780144000357 / 9780670999415) [V]; *The Biology of Enlightenment: Unpublished Conversations… 1967–71* (HarperCollins India 2010, ISBN 9789350290095) [V]; *The Penguin U.G. Krishnamurti Reader* (Penguin 2007) [K].
- **K. Chandrasekhar (Bangalore)** — notebooks that became *Stopped in Our Tracks* (trans./ed. Narayana Moorty) [V]; appears in archive.org recording with Dr K.B. Rao & Brahmachari [V].
- **Suguna & Lakshmi / Poornakutee** — Suguna is host at Poornakutee in *The Sage and the Housewife* ch. 7 [V]; Brahmachari in Bangalore circle (Parvathi blog) [V].
- **J.S.R.L. Narayana Moorty** — Monterey Peninsula College; maintains WELL archive; translator/editor of Stopped in Our Tracks; co-editor *Thought Is Your Enemy* and *No Way Out* [V].
- **Antony Paul Frank Noronha** — co-editor *Thought Is Your Enemy*, *No Way Out*; UG's host in Delhi; "Sowmya Noronha" is copyright holder of *The Sage and the Housewife* (released) [V]. (Not Frederick Noronha the Goa journalist.)
- **Terry Newland** — editor *Mind Is a Myth* (Dinesh 1988; Sentient 2007) [V].
- **Rodney Arms** — editor *The Mystique of Enlightenment* (Dinesh 1982) [V].
- **Ellen Chrystal** — transcribed/edited *The Courage to Stand Alone* (Plover Press NY 1997) [V].
- **Henk Schonewille (Netherlands)** — recorded ~24 h of UG's 1982 Amsterdam talks; released "Give Up" 3-cassette series (4½ h); foreword Amsterdam July 1995; source of Courage to Stand Alone [V].
- **Shanta Kelker** — *The Sage and the Housewife* (Sowmya 1990) [V].
- **Peter Maverick** — 2005 compilation cited on Wikiquote [V] (*The Natural State*, Smriti Books [K]).
