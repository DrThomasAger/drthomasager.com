Born of Fear

Collection on a topicfear2026-11-04 · composed with claude-opus-5-5 from the verified quotationsEdit this page

U.G. Krishnamurti's words on fear, gathered from the books of his conversations and checked against their sources. He did not treat fear as a problem to be solved. He found it at the root of the self, of the search, of God, and of the wish to be free of fear itself. The order here moves from where fear begins, through what it makes and the freedom no one wants, to the body that has no part in it.

Where fear begins

the very birth of thought creates fear

Mind is a Myth, Ch. V We Have Created This Jungle Society

What you call 'yourself' is fear. The 'you' is born out of fear; it lives in fear, functions in fear and dies in fear.

No Way Out

What fear makes

There is no power outside of man. Man has created God out of fear. So the problem is fear and not God.

The Mystique of Enlightenment

it is the mind that has created God out of fear

Mind is a Myth, p. 31

It is fear that makes you believe that you are living and that you will be dead. What we do not want is the fear to come to an end.

The Mystique of Enlightenment

The freedom no one wants

We don't want to be free from fear. All that we want to do is to play games with it and talk about freeing ourselves from fear.

The Mystique of Enlightenment

Fear is the very thing that you do not want to be free from.

No Way Out

The fact is that we don't want to be free. What is responsible for our problems is the fear of losing what we have and what we know.

No Way Out

The end of fear

You see, you love fear. The ending of fear is death, and you don't want THAT to happen.

Mind is a Myth, I

The body

The body cannot be afraid of death. The movement that is created by society or culture is what does not want to come to an end.

No Way Out
