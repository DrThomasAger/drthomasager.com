[Intro]

[Zanj cymbals, nashik dhol, tasha snares build]
[Crowd chant]
Don't follow me, I'm lost.

[Shehnai]
[Said kindly]
I am not trying to sell anything here.
I have nothing to give; you have nothing to take.

[Verse 1]
[Male deep]
I have had to search
and it is the search itself
which has been choking me
and keeping me out of my natural state.
[Tasha snares]
You have to liberate yourself.
The fact is that we don't want to be free.
A real guru, if there is one,
frees you from himself.

[Pre-Chorus]
[Woman]
it has to dawn on you
that it is not leading you anywhere
Courage is not an instrument or quality
you can use to get somewhere.

[Chorus]
[Chorus of men Shout together]
[Music stays loud]
The search ends with the realization
that there is no such thing as enlightenment.
[Mantra chorus]
[Crowd chant, call and response over the dhol]
Transformation, moksha, liberation,
Transformation, moksha, liberation,
Transformation, moksha, liberation,
Transformation, moksha, liberation,
[Chorus of men Shout together]
The search ends with the realization
that there is no such thing as enlightenment.

[Post-Chorus]
[Woman]
the same old nonsense repeating itself
You have to liberate yourself.

[Dhol tasha break]
[Bass drop]

[Verse 2]
[Male deep]
your teacher must go, it doesn't matter who
If anyone thinks he can help you,
he will inevitably mislead you…
A guru is one who tells you
to throw away all the crutches
that we have been made to believe
are essential for our survival.
[Woman]
as long as you depend upon somebody
for solving your problems,
so long you remain helpless

[Pre-Chorus]
[Said kindly]
all gurus are welfare organizations
providing petty experiences to their followers
All your experiences, all your meditations,
all your prayer, all that you do, is self-centred.

[Chorus]
[Chorus of men Shout together]
[Music stays loud]

that there is no such thing as enlightenment.

Transformation, moksha, liberation,
Transformation, moksha, liberation,
Transformation, moksha, liberation,
Transformation, moksha, liberation,
[Chorus of men Shout together]
The search ends with the realization
that there is no such thing as enlightenment.

[Shehnai]
[Festival brass band]

[Bridge]
[Male deep]
You are trying to enforce peace through violence.
Yoga, meditation,
prayers, mantras, are all violent techniques.
[Woman]
Your thinking endlessly about being free from sorrow is only more material for sorrow.
I am only interested in describing this state,
in clearing away the occultation and mystification
in which those people in the 'holy business'
have shrouded the whole thing.

[Dhol tasha break]
[Bass drop]

[Verse 3]
[Festival brass band, crowd claps on two]
[Said kindly]
you arrive seeking understanding,
while I am only interested
in making it crystal clear
that there is nothing to understand.
Man has created God out of fear.
[Male deep]
the courage to be on your own, to stand
on your two solid feet,
is something which cannot be given by somebody.
All I can guarantee you is that
as long as you are searching for happiness,
you will remain unhappy.

[Final Chorus]
[Chorus of men Shout together]
[Music stays loud]

that there is no such thing as enlightenment.

that there is no such thing as enlightenment.
