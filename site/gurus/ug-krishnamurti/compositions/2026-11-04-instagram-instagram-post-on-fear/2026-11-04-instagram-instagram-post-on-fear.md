QUOTE
What you call 'yourself' is fear. The 'you' is born out of fear; it lives in fear, functions in fear and dies in fear.

CAPTION
Most of us treat fear as something that happens to us, a visitor we would like to send away. U.G. Krishnamurti turned this around. He did not say that you have fear. He said that the one who wants to be free of it is made of the same stuff. Every plan to overcome fear is drawn up by fear itself, which is why the plans never quite work. He offered no method and no comfort here, and he would not let the sentence become a new belief. Try reading it slowly, once, and then watch what moves in you as you read. Whatever rises to agree or to argue may be the very thing he was pointing at.

SOURCE
U.G. Krishnamurti, No Way Out
