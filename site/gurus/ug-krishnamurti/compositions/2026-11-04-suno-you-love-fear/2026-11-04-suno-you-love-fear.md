# You Love Fear

STYLE
festival anthem, hype anime opening, 808 sub-bass, hard 808s, blown out kicks, deep bass beats, recurring theme melody, upright bass, flowing melodies, hypnotic dreamy layers, lush synth pads, chorus, chanting, mantra, kirtan, stark spoken male verses, women's chorus answering, slow build to a heavy drop, the words always clear above the beat

LYRICS
[ALL LYRICS MUST BE SAID CORRECTLY AT ALL TIMES]

[Intro]
[Male deep]
[Said kindly]
the very birth of thought creates fear

[Verse 1]
[Male deep]
What you call 'yourself' is fear.
The 'you' is born out of fear;
it lives in fear,
functions in fear
and dies in fear.

[Pre-Chorus]
[Woman]
It is fear that makes you believe that you are living
and that you will be dead.

[Chorus]
[Chorus of women Sing together]
You love fear.
The ending of fear is death,
and you don't want that to happen.
You love fear.
The ending of fear is death,
and you don't want that to happen.

[Bass drop]
[Music stays loud]

[Verse 2]
[Male deep]
There is no power outside of man.
Man has created God out of fear.
So the problem is fear and not God.
[Said kindly]
it is the mind that has created God out of fear

[Pre-Chorus]
[Woman]
We don't want to be free from fear.
All that we want to do is to play games with it
and talk about freeing ourselves from fear.

[Chorus]
[Chorus of women Sing together]
You love fear.
The ending of fear is death,
and you don't want that to happen.
Fear is the very thing that you do not want to be free from.

[Bridge]
[Male deep]
[Said kindly]
The fact is that we don't want to be free.
What is responsible for our problems
is the fear of losing what we have and what we know.

[Bass drop]
[Music stays loud]

[Verse 3]
[Woman]
The body cannot be afraid of death.
The movement that is created by society or culture
is what does not want to come to an end.
[Male deep]
The body changes from one state into another
as recycling of matter.

[Final Chorus]
[Chorus of women Sing together]
The body cannot be afraid of death.
in that sense the body is immortal
The body cannot be afraid of death.
in that sense the body is immortal

[Outro]
[Male deep]
[Said kindly]
the very birth of thought creates fear
