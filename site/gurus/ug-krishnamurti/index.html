The life

1918-07-09

Uppaluri Gopala Krishnamurti is born in Masulipatnam (Machilipatnam), Madras Presidency, into a Telugu-speaking Brahmin family.

Masulipatnam, India
1918-07

His mother Bharati dies seven days after his birth; he is taken in by his maternal grandparents, T.G. Krishnamurti (a wealthy Gudivada lawyer and staunch Theosophist) and Durgamma, and brought up in Gudivada.

Gudivada, India
c. 1919

His father remarries soon after the mother's death and leaves the boy entirely in the grandparents' care; UG later said he never really knew his father.

Gudivada, India
1925-12

According to Mahesh Bhatt ('A Life', ch. 2), the boy is taken to the Theosophical Society's Golden Jubilee convention at Adyar, where Annie Besant speaks to him and C. Jinarajadasa, standing behind her, takes note of him.

Adyar, Madras, India
1932-12

Attends the Theosophical Society annual convention at Adyar and meets Annie Besant; Jinarajadasa presents him the book 'I Promise' -- described on the official site as the beginning of his sadhana.

Adyar, Madras, India
c. 1932-1939

Between roughly 14 and 21 years of age spends seven summers, off and on, at Swami Sivananda's ashram in Rishikesh, practising classical yoga and meditation and performing tapas in Himalayan caves.

Rishikesh, India
1939

Enrols at the University of Madras to study psychology, philosophy and the sciences; after his grandfather's death he leaves without completing a degree (some accounts credit him with bachelor's degrees in philosophy and psychology).

Madras, India
1939-12

Meets Ramana Maharshi at Tiruvannamalai and asks 'Can you give me what you have?'; the reply 'I can give, but can you take it?' marks, in UG's words, the point at which 'all that India stood for went out of my system'.

Tiruvannamalai, India
1939

Official key-events table lists a 'first trip overseas' in 1939.

Abroad
c. 1941

Formally joins the Theosophical Society (Wikipedia says he became a member in his teens; another account gives 1941) and begins his long involvement with Adyar after the Ramana meeting.

Adyar, Madras, India
