What he said about publishing at all

Dinesh Vaghela's publisher's note to Mind is a Myth quotes him when the idea of publishing excerpts was put to him. "Why bother publishing my conversations. It has not helped you, and it is not going to help anybody else." The publisher went ahead anyway. The first book had been The Mystique of Enlightenment, which U.G. called the "Mistake of Enlightenment".

How the release has been treated

Wikisource hosts the conversation books under the notice that the work is in the public domain worldwide because it has been so released by the copyright holder. The Internet Archive carries scans under Public Domain Mark 1.0. The WELL placed all the works in their entirety for free download. Commercial editions continue (Sentient 2001–2002, Penguin India 2007, HarperCollins 2018, e-artnow 2021–2022), which the statement also permits.

The release gives away authorship itself. The permission to "even claim authorship" removes the attribution interest that most free licences keep. It is not a legal template. It is a sentence in his voice where a copyright line goes, and no estate has contested it since there is no estate. He founded nothing, left no will and no property, and asked that no rituals follow his death.

How it was gathered

The gathering session's network policy refused direct connections to every hosting site (the WELL, Wikisource, Wikiquote, the Internet Archive, YouTube, the quote aggregators), so no file was downloaded. Seven parallel researchers swept web search, one per medium, and every item here is what those searches surfaced, with the URL each one gave. The data files live beside this page in the repository and can be regenerated from them.
