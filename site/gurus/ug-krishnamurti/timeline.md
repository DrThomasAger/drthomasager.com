# U.G. Krishnamurti — life timeline

For placing every recording, photograph and transcript in time. Dates
from the standard accounts (Mahesh Bhatt, *U.G. Krishnamurti: A Life*;
the WELL biography pages; Wikipedia; Mukunda Rao). Items marked (?) are
given differently by different accounts.

| Date | Event | Place |
|---|---|---|
| 9 July 1918 | Born into a Telugu Brahmin family. Mother dies seven days after his birth, saying the child was born to a destiny of enormous spiritual proportions. Raised by maternal grandfather T.G. Krishnamurti, a Theosophist and lawyer. | Masulipatnam (Machilipatnam), Andhra Pradesh |
| 1920s–1930s | Childhood under Theosophical and orthodox Brahmin influence. Sacred-thread ceremony. Grandfather's daily meditation hall. | Gudivada |
| c. 1932–1939 | Seven summers of yoga and meditation with Swami Sivananda in the Himalayas (from about age fourteen). Later says he did everything in the books and found nothing. | Rishikesh |
| c. 1939 | Meets Ramana Maharshi; asks whether what he has can be given. Ramana answers "I can give it, but can you take it?" He says this turned him back on himself. | Tiruvannamalai |
| 1939–1943 | University of Madras, philosophy and psychology, leaves without a degree. | Madras |
| 1935–1960s | Theosophical Society member, speaker and lecturer for the Society in India and Europe. Meets Jiddu Krishnamurti c. 1953 and has conversations with him over several years; breaks off in 1961. | Adyar, Madras; Europe |
| 1943 | Marries Kusuma Kumari. Four children follow; first son Vasant later has polio. | Madras |
| 1955–1961 | Travels to the United States for his son's polio treatment. Lectures in the US. Money runs out. | USA |
| 1961 | Leaves family in India; wanders in London destitute, cooking in a Ramakrishna Mission kitchen; sleeps in Hyde Park. The Theosophist-era life ends. | London |
| 1963 | Kusuma Kumari dies in India (per the people registry; some accounts place it later). | India |
| 1961–1963 | Drifts to Paris, then Geneva. Arrives at the Indian consulate in Geneva penniless; meets Valentine de Kerven, who becomes his lifelong companion and supporter. | Geneva |
| 1963–1967 | Lives with Valentine in Saanen, visiting the Krishnamurti talks there. Years he later calls a "hibernation". | Saanen, Switzerland |
| 13 July 1967 (?) | Hears J. Krishnamurti speak at Saanen and finds Krishnamurti is describing his own state; walks out. | Saanen |
| 13–20 July 1967 | The week of the "calamity" beginning on his forty-ninth birthday: seven days of physical changes he later describes, the body's "death" and return, ending on 20 July. | Saanen |
| 1967–1970s | Lives between Saanen/Gstaad (summers) and India (winters), conversations begin to be recorded on cassette by visitors. | Gstaad; Bangalore |
| 1982 | *The Mystique of Enlightenment* published by Dinesh Vaghela, Goa (imprint given as Dinesh Publications, or his Cemetile Corporation; 5,000 copies), ed. Rodney Arms, with the no-copyright statement. Amsterdam conversations recorded by Henk Schonewille (later *The Courage to Stand Alone*). | Goa; Amsterdam |
| 1983–1984 | Conversations in India, Switzerland and California that become *Mind is a Myth*. | Bangalore, Gstaad, California |
| 1988 | *Mind is a Myth* published, ed. Terry Newland. | Dinesh Publications |
| 1980s–1990s | Winters in Bangalore at Poornakutee, the house of Suguna and Chandrasekhar; the "Bangalore conversations" video-recorded. Visits to Yercaud, Mysore, Kodaikanal, Bombay. | Bangalore |
| 20 January 1991 | Valentine de Kerven dies in Bangalore, aged about ninety. | Bangalore |
| 1990–1991 | *Thought is Your Enemy* and *No Way Out* published, ed. Frank Noronha with J.S.R.L. Narayana Moorty. | Sowmya Publishers, Bangalore |
| 1992 | Mahesh Bhatt publishes *U.G. Krishnamurti: A Life*. Second edition of *The Mystique of Enlightenment*. | Penguin India |
| 1980s–2000s | Travels yearly between Bangalore, Gstaad, California (Mill Valley, Carmel, Palm Springs, Seaside), Amsterdam, Cologne, Sydney, Vallecrosia. Video recorded by Julie Thayer and others. | worldwide |
| 1997 | *The Courage to Stand Alone* published, ed. Ellen J. Chrystal, transcribed from the 1982 Henk Schonewille tapes. | Plover Press / Sentient |
| 2001–2002 | Sentient Publications (Boulder) reissues *The Mystique of Enlightenment*, *Mind is a Myth*, *The Courage to Stand Alone*, *No Way Out*. | Boulder, Colorado |
| 2005 | *The Natural State*, compiled by Peter Maverick. | |
| 2005–2006 | Louis Brawley travels with him in his final years (later *Goner*). | |
| January–February 2007 | Slips and falls in Vallecrosia; bedridden for seven weeks. Mid-February dictates "My Swan Song" to Louis Brawley. | Vallecrosia, Liguria, Italy |
| 22 March 2007 | Dies at Vallecrosia, aged 88, Mahesh Bhatt and two friends present. Body cremated by Bhatt the next day; no rituals, no will, no property. | Vallecrosia |
| 2007 | *The Penguin U.G. Krishnamurti Reader*, ed. Mukunda Rao. | Penguin India |
| 2009 | Mahesh Bhatt, *A Taste of Life: The Last Days of U.G. Krishnamurti*. | |
| 2011–2012 | Louis Brawley, *Goner: The Final Travels of UG Krishnamurti* (Non-Duality Press). | |
| 2018 | *UG Says: Everyday Thoughts from UG Krishnamurti*, ed. Arun Babani. | HarperCollins India |
| 2021–2022 | e-artnow collected editions (*Essential Works*, *Collected Works*). | |
