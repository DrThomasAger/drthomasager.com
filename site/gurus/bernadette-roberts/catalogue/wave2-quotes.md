## Recovered chapter / contents lists

- **The Experience of No-Self** (Shambhala 1982/84; SUNY rev. 1993): Introduction; Part I "The Journey" (ch. 1 opens in the monastery chapel by the sea); Part II analytical chapters. Searches did not surface a verified list of chapter titles; the requested titles ("the first days", "the passage", "the Great Passage", "the doorway", "the marketplace", "the stillpoint", "the eye of the cyclone", "what remains", "the seeing", "the body", "silence", "the journey's end") could not be confirmed from any result.
- **The Path to No-Self: Life at the Center** (Shambhala 1985; SUNY 1991, foreword Thomas Keating): Preface; a six-stage account over ~20 years beginning with the Dark Night of the Spirit and ending in no-self. No chapter titles recovered.
- **What Is Self? A Study of the Spiritual Journey in Terms of Consciousness** (Goens 1989; Sentient 2005 with forewords by Jeff Shore and Ric Williams). Sections on the reflexive mechanism, the unconscious (Jung), Buddhism, Christ, Trinity; p.127 cited for the "mystery of Christ exploded" line. A separate short "What Is Self? — A Research Paper" (with Preface) circulates as a PDF on o-meditation.com.
- **The Real Christ** (2017, ~380 pp.): no chapter list recovered; author's warning on her Books page: readers who hold the historical Jesus is God should stop reading.
- **Contemplative: Autobiography of the Early Years** (2008): birth to age seventeen; opens "This book is an account of my spiritual journey from birth to seventeen."
- **The Christian Contemplative Journey: Essays on the Path** (2007, 161 pp.; 2017 ed., ~305 pp., ContemplativeChristians.com). Essays per her Books page: The Spiritual Journey Recapitulates the History of Religion; Cessation of Self; Nonduality; Mystical Theology and No-Self; Image and Likeness; Apatheia; Means-End; The Ox Herding Pictures; The Eucharist (A Christian Path); "What is Self?" (a research paper); Christ as the Definition of "Non-duality". (Eleven titles in the 2017 listing, not eight.)
- **Other writings**: *A Passage Through Self* (talk/booklet with circle diagrams); *Forcing the Fit* (2008 spiral-bound manuscript answering A.H. Almaas, James Marion and Jim Arraj); "Reply to James Arraj's comments" (blog, June 2008); "Nonduality as a Definition of Christ" (SAND 2015 conference paper); autobiographical sketch in Ullman & Reichenberg-Ullman, *Mystics, Masters, Saints and Sages* (2001).

## Attribution conflicts and cautions

1. **"In the absence of self, all that remained was an immovable stillness, an unbreakable, unfeeling silence."** — No Mind's Land (2020) and Sat Sangha Salon give it as the autobiographical sketch she wrote for *Mystics, Masters, Saints and Sages* (2001); an Enlightened People commenter says it is from *The Experience of No-Self*. Filed as unassigned.
2. **"When there is no longer a cyclone, there is no longer an eye."** (already harvested) — AZQuotes credits *The Path to No-Self* p.47; Goodreads credits *The Christian Contemplative Journey*. Not re-added.
3. **AZQuotes p.47 (Path to No-Self)** passage on personality as a way of expression: the pdfcoffee mirror shows a "47" page marker near the "underground explosion" passage instead, so the AZQuotes page number is doubtful. Wording of the personality passage was not recovered.
4. **AZQuotes p.155 (The Experience of No-Self)** for "The assumption that the egoless condition ... is a great mistake" conflicts with its other listing at *Path to No-Self* p.34 (already harvested under Path).
5. **"a path exists between the transcendence of the ego ..."** and **"once we have adjusted to the unitive state ..."** are publisher (SUNY) description sentences that may be editorial rather than Roberts's own.
6. **"there is no such 'thing' as 'the unconscious'..."** — summary says "a separate essay"; could be *What Is Self?* (Jung section) or the essays volume. Filed under *What Is Self?*.
7. **"Where Buddha described the experience, Christ manifested it without words ..."** — Goodreads author page; book inferred as the essays volume from neighbouring entries. Could be *What Is Self?*.
8. **Jeff Shore foreword** items (5) are Shore's words, not Roberts's; kept because the brief asked for them, marked *NOT Roberts* in notes.
9. **Ken Wilber**: no Wilber sentence quoting Roberts surfaced. **Jim Marion**: his Shalom Place dialogue with Philip St. Romain mentions her and calls aspects of her teaching "problematic" but quotes no Roberts sentence; nothing added.
10. **Spiritual Life** (ICS journal): no review located. **Reddit / Substack**: no post quoting her verbatim surfaced; dancingparadox.substack.com was named but its content not shown.
11. **Bodian interview**: "to eliminate any possibility for misunderstanding" is Bodian's preamble, possibly his wording.
12. **sloww.co** "25 quotes" page states its quotes are "primarily from publicly available videos"; only paraphrased themes were visible (self diminishes as one becomes fuller of God; reformation vs transformation; unknowing to knowing), so nothing from it was captured verbatim.
