# Bernadette Roberts — audio and video

Every recording of her voice or image found by web search on 2026-10-25,
plus readings and talks about her. Bernadette Roberts (1931 – 27 November
2017), the Carmelite-trained contemplative; namesakes excluded (the
"Reseo Conference 2017: Interview with Bernadette Roberts" on YouTube is
an opera-education professional, and the Tarot Tribe podcast guest is
Nadine Bernadette Roberts).

Machine-readable copy: `scratchpad/json-br/av.json` (18 items; schema in
GATHERING.md). Confidence: S = confirmed against a direct description;
lead = location known, content unseen.

## 1. The two authorized recordings (DVD, sold by Bernadette's Friends)

Her student circle's blog states that these are the only authorized
recordings and that "there are no authorized videos or audio recordings
of Bernadette on YouTube or any other site"; everything else below is
either an unauthorized excerpt of these, a broadcast interview, or a
reading by someone else. Masters are held by Bernadette's Friends
(bernadettesfriends.blogspot.com, bernadetteroberts.blogspot.com), the
group that sold the sets on her behalf and announced her death.

| Title | Recorded | Format | Length | Content | Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Essence of Christian Mysticism** (recorded retreat) | undated; she gave the retreat annually for c. 30 years until it was recorded and distributed, after which she stopped giving it | DVD set, 4 files + handouts | c. 9 h | The retreat for practising Christian contemplatives who had read her first three books; she called it a prerequisite for *The Real Christ*. Covers the passage through self, Christ, Trinity, Eucharist. | S |
| **A Passage Through Self** ("the circles course") | 1994, on VHS; transferred to 2 DVDs | 2-DVD set | 2.5–3 h | Circle diagrams mapping ego-self to True Self in union, dark night of the spirit, unitive state, "marketplace" stage, final falling away of self. A Shalom Place reviewer notes tape hiss from the VHS original. Prerequisite viewing for the retreat. | S |

Ordering page: https://bernadetteroberts.blogspot.com/p/dvdcd.html
(US price included shipping; Canadian buyers reported customs charges).
Whether sets are still shipped after her death in 2017 is not confirmed.

## 2. Broadcast interview (her voice)

| Title | Date | Host | Format | Length | Where | Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Passion of Grace**, New Dimensions Radio program 2276 | interview 1 Aug 1991 | Michael Toms | public-radio interview; MP3; also sold as a 58-min unabridged audiobook (Google Play, Barnes & Noble, Everand) | 58 min | https://programs.newdimensions.org/products/the-passion-of-grace-with-bernadette-roberts | S |

Topics: the nature of self and its relation to God, grace, the spiritual
value of suffering, the Virgin Mary, prayer. Master held by New Dimensions
Foundation. This is the only broadcast interview of her located; the
well-known Stephan Bodian interview (Yoga Journal, Nov/Dec 1986, reprinted
in *Timeless Visions, Healing Voices*, 1991) exists only as text.
