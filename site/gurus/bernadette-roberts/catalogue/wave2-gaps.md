# Bernadette Roberts — wave 2: gap research

Scope: Bernadette Roberts (1931–2017), Catholic contemplative, author of *The Experience of No-Self*; every other person of that name excluded. Compiled 2026-10-25 from 46 web searches (extended mode); no page could be fetched, so "S" means a search snippet or page description states it, "lead" means inferred or unconfirmed. Machine-readable copy: `scratchpad/json-br/wave2-gaps.json` (60 rows).

## 1. The 2021 "free online" release of the retreat recordings

**What the notice says.** The Aquarian Catholic Spiritual Community page "Bernadette Roberts Talks Available" (Sept 2021) reports that the recorded retreat talks she gave over her lifetime "are now available to view at no cost", links to the viewing site, warns that the talks are long, and recommends re-watching with notes. The retreat named is *The Essence of Christian Mysticism* (Trinity, Christ, Faith). Search snippets never expose the outbound link.

**What it is not.** Nothing on bernadetteroberts.blogspot.com announces a free release: its Recorded Talks page still lists the DVD set (*The Essence of Christian Mysticism*, "nine hours of talks in 4 files, plus handouts", headed Talk #1–#4; *A Passage Through Self*, 1994 VHS origin) and the home page still states that no videos or audio recordings of her on YouTube or any other site are authorised. No Google Drive, Vimeo, Dropbox or blog-hosted copy surfaced, and no numbered full upload ("Talk 1", "Day 1", "Part 1") exists on YouTube.

**What it most likely is (lead).** The only free viewing route that exists is the unauthorised YouTube series of DVD excerpts titled "Bernadette Roberts on …", gathered in the playlist "Bernadette Roberts" (PLni-GlNUOJfZQx47wittRX-i63CvNV2E8, description: teachings of "the most original and controversial mystic of all time"). Clip descriptions point to www.bernadetterobertsisnotmyguru.com, the site of Phoenix Kennedy, author of five books on her (reviewers say written against her repeated requests). The posthumous "free" release is therefore almost certainly this fan upload, not a release by Bernadette's Friends, and the earlier chronology line "her site announces…" should be corrected: the announcement was the Aquarian page's, not her site's. Confirmation needs one fetch of the Aquarian post's link.

## 3. DVD sales and masters

- bernadetteroberts.blogspot.com was set up by **Ramesh Balakrishnan** and managed "for so many years" by **Patricia (Pat) Masters**, who died 31 May 2024 (announced on the blog). The friends have known her since the early 1980s. ALS was diagnosed a year before her death on 27 Nov 2017.
- The Recorded Talks page still offers the DVD set; bernadettesfriends.blogspot.com has an "Ordering Information" section (last retreat dates seen: 11–13 Oct 2013 and 28–30 Mar 2014, Fullerton CA, max 13, $350, prerequisites: first three books + *A Passage Through Self* DVD). Whether orders are fulfilled after Masters' death is unknown; no holder of the masters is named anywhere.
- ContemplativeChristians.com Publishing: P.O. Box 164202, Austin, TX 78716 (copyright pages, June and Nov 2018 printings); *The Real Christ* first printing Feb 2017, ISBN 978-0-692-85115-9. No operator name, email or Wayback capture found.

## 4. New Camaldoli, SAND 2015, the Praetorius paper

- **New Camaldoli Hermitage:** no recording, talk or mention found; only a DBpedia backlink from her Wikipedia article. Negative.
- **SAND 2015:** paper "Non-duality as a Definition of Christ", presented at the SAND Conference, San Jose, 22 Oct 2015; PDF on her blog's "Papers for Download" page (research-paper.html) and on o-meditation.com; later the essay "Christ as the Definition of Non-duality" in *The Christian Contemplative Journey* (2017). SAND archive has a speaker page (archive.scienceandnonduality.com/speakers/bernadette-roberts/); no session video surfaced on the SAND channel. Filming: unconfirmed.
- **"What Is Self? A Research Paper":** published in the *Journal of Consciousness Studies* vol. 23 no. 1–2 (Jan/Feb 2016), p. 80, Imprint Academic, in the section of "target papers by spiritual masters". Preface: written at the request of Dr Nini Praetorius, Docent Professor, University of Copenhagen, for her project on a new approach to studying the self; thesis "self is the cause of individuation". Free PDF on her blog and o-meditation.com. **Closed.**
