## Conflicts and corrections

1. **Ullman sketch, not The Experience of No-Self.** `q45d56e01`, `qef5dd361`, `q889cddbc`, `q5ed0c359` ("it was the moment 'I' entered a great silence and never returned", "In the absence of self, all that remained was an immovable stillness...") come from the autobiographical statement she wrote for Ullman & Reichenberg-Ullman, *Mystics, Masters, Saints and Sages* (2001), reproduced on enlightened-people.com, o-meditation.com, westernmystics and No Mind's Land. Same sketch yields the new verbatim line "Truly, God is all that exists–all, of course, but the self." which is the real source of St. Romain's "God is everything that exists ... except the self" (`q336b7ef3`).
2. **The Experience of No-Self vs The Path to No-Self (aggregators).** `q066e6835` ("Instead of the usual unlocalized centre of myself...") is confirmed in the Perlego primary excerpt of *ENS*; azquotes' *Path* p.47/p.15 citation is wrong. Conversely `q19a3d246` and `q78be2fac` are confirmed in the *Path* preface (pdfcoffee full text, Google Books). `qa2082e12`/`qfb8ae3e7`/`q03c1375a` are one sentence (full text recovered) that aggregators place in the *Path* p.5; innerexplorations' ENS attribution for the fragment is the outlier.
3. **Stillpoint / cyclone passage.** `qfd33296f`, `q885f4c3e`, `qbbc255fb`, `qaf2a7bb7`, `qfd5bc008`, `qc5b19862` all belong to one passage. Its only confirmed host is the 1986 Bodian interview (Yoga Journal Nov/Dec 1986; *Timeless Visions, Healing Voices* 1991); Goodreads tags the same passage to *The Christian Contemplative Journey: Essays on the Path*; azquotes' *ENS* p.155 / *Path* p.47 citations are unsupported. Wording corrections: "And when there is no longer a cyclone, there is **also** no longer an eye."; "a refuge from the swirling cyclone of **endless** suffering"; "a spot or center of calm, imperturbability, and non-movement".
4. **Interview sentences wrongly left unassigned.** `q32025793` ("self" and "consciousness" ... "No-self, then, means no-consciousness.") and `q0608ea9b` ("As I see it, without a personal God, the Buddhist must have a much stronger faith in the 'unconditioned and unbegotten'") are verbatim in the Bodian interview.
5. **The Real Christ, not the essay.** `q196b5e82` "Christ is the eternal oneness of two natures, divine and human." is on every host as a sentence of *The Real Christ* (full sentence: "Christ is not the oneness of two persons, two individuals or two beings (a divine being and a human being), rather, Christ is ..."). Note hosts date *The Real Christ* to 2012 (self-published), not 2017.
6. **Paraphrases presented as her text.** `q64642b84` ("a silence that was so total as to evoke fear, annihilation, point of no return") is the No Mind's Land blogger's condensation; the primary sentence (Perlego) is "But I must preface this account by saying that on previous occasions, I had come upon a pervasive silence of the faculties so total as to give rise to subtle apprehensions of fear." `qafca4e1c` is the josephbray/No Mind's Land summary. `qbb7bfd78` (Eckhart) is a Medium blogger's sentence around her phrase "beyond union, beyond self and God".
7. **Not her words (10).** Jeff Shore's foreword to *What Is Self?*: `q0b54eb65` (only "...that which remains when there is no self", cited to *ENS* p.140, is hers), `qee0b27d3`, `qa1d5eefd`, `q747d1ceb`, `q145166d9`, `q7c229bd4`; third-person editorial summary of Part I: `q0656d525` (and `q00062066` is the same third-person prose, kept as corroborated-primary with a caution); Bodian's introduction: `qf4de088e`; reviewers: `q674005ff` (melotree-star.win), `q98059cf9` (spiritualteachers.org profile).
8. **Not found.** `qbe7feed4` ("a final and complete loss-of-self is not achieved by ecstasy, spiritual marriage...") and `q9bc5472b` ("the contemplative must journey alone") have no host; keep as paraphrase, low confidence.
9. **Single-host primary lines.** `qffee5269` (Eucharist sentence) did not surface in any snippet; it rests solely on the earlier archive.org read. `qdde04e58` ("How it Works") is truncated on its only host.

## Recovered 1986 Bodian interview sentences

Seven online copies were consulted through search snippets: realization.org, spiritualteachers.org, awakeningtoreality.com, o-meditation.com (Sat Sangha Salon), standinginanopenfield.wordpress.com, taooflightyoga.com, katolisitas.org (Indonesian commentary quoting the English). Sentences recovered verbatim or near-verbatim:

- “Paradoxical though it may seem, the passage through consciousness or self moves contrary to self, rubs it the wrong way - and in the end, will even rub it out.” — S/verbatim; Confirmed verbatim on the Awakening to Reality and spiritualteachers.org reposts.
- “As I see it, without a personal God, the Buddhist must have a much stronger faith in the "unconditioned and unbegotten".” — S/verbatim; Full sentence of catalogue fragment q0608ea9b; confirmed on four reposts.
- “"personal God" is any subjective experience of the divine.” — T/fragment; From the same answer: consciousness is the human faculty for experiencing the divine.
- “Without a personal, subjective self, we could not even speak of an impersonal, non-subjective God; one is just relative to the other.” — S/verbatim
- “If we tried to do so, we would either end up with another side, or the whole coin would collapse, leaving no center at all.” — T/verbatim; Sentence adjacent to the 'divine center - the coin' passage (q147cc879); the antecedent of 'to do so' (separating the two sides of the coin) is not in the snippet.
- “No-self, then, means no-consciousness.” — S/verbatim; Complete sentence; preceded by 'the terms "self" and "consciousness" express the same experiences' (q32025793).
- “the storms, crises and sufferings of life are a way of finding the eye” — T/fragment; Companion line of the stillpoint/cyclone passage; quote sites vary the exact text.
- “Christ articulated the eye in more positive terms as the "Kingdom of God" or the "Spirit within"” — T/fragment; Stillpoint passage; also tagged by Goodreads to The Christian Contemplative Journey: Essays on the Path.
- “Buddha's "emptiness" or "void", a refuge from the swirling cyclone of endless suffering” — T/fragment; Corrects catalogue qbbc255fb ('endless suffering').
- “authors come up with various stages, depending on the criteria they are using” — T/fragment; From the stage-theory answer (Teresa's seven mansions given as an example).
- “it is always someone else's retrospective view of his or her own journey, which may not include our own experiences or insights” — T/fragment; Same answer as 'I don't think we should get locked into any stage theory'.
- “our obligation is to be true to our own insights, our own inner light” — T/fragment; Search summary: 'This phrase appears in several reprints of the interview.'
- “the terms "purgative", "illuminative", and "unitive" (often used of the contemplative path) do not refer to discrete stages, but to a way of travel” — T/fragment; Opening of her answer to the first question (the first three stages of the Christian contemplative life).
- “once we come to the state of oneness, we can go no further with the inward journey” — T/fragment; Continues (paraphrase) that the movement then turns outward: 'the center is expanding outward'.
- (Bodian, interviewer) “Could you talk briefly about the first three stages of the Christian contemplative life as you experienced them” — T/fragment; NOT her words: Stephan Bodian's opening question (recorded for context).

Already-catalogued interview lines confirmed verbatim on multiple reposts: `qc464e660`, `qa8726644` (Buddha quotation), `q147cc879` (the coin), `qd595c753`, `qca48c952`, `q8cae5ac0`.
