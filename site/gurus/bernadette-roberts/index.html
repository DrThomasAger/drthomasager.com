Bernadette Roberts

1931, California (parents' home in Hollywood) · 27 November 2017, at home in Southern California (Laguna Beach per Wikipedia)

A Carmelite-trained contemplative who left the cloister at the monastery's blessing, raised four children, and wrote of a passage beyond the unitive state in which the self fell away entirely, which she called no-self.

The life

1931

Born in California to devout Catholic parents; the family home she later returns to is in Hollywood, Los Angeles. (Site tradition: Los Angeles; Wikipedia gives only 'California'.)

Los Angeles (Hollywood), California
c. 1935

Childhood in a home of 'extraordinary love of God and Catholic faith'; the early spiritual experiences of these years are the subject of Contemplative: Autobiography of the Early Years (birth to seventeen). Her later turn, per one reviewer, was 'much to the chagrin of her parents'.

Los Angeles, California
1949-01

Enters the Monastery of Discalced Carmelite Nuns, Alhambra, California, aged seventeen.

Alhambra, California
c. 1950

The 'transforming process' and the unitive state, reached in Carmel in her late teens and twenties; the no-self event comes 'some 25 years after the transforming process' (Bodian interview) or 'some 20 years after leaving the convent' (summaries). Her cloister years are described in The Path to No-Self.

Alhambra, California
c. 1957

Leaves the cloister with the permission of her superiors to live the contemplative life outside. Length of stay given as eight and a half years (Wikipedia; implies mid-1957), nine years (SUNY blurb, Bodian; 1958) or ten years (Hridaya, Enlightened People; 1959).

Alhambra, California
c. 1957

Enters the University of Utah as a pre-medical student for three years.

Salt Lake City, Utah
c. 1960

Returns to her parents' home in Hollywood and takes a degree in philosophy at the University of Southern California. Degrees are given variously: a philosophy degree (Wikipedia); BA Philosophy and later MA Early Childhood Education, both USC (spiritualteachers.org); 'a graduate degree in education' (SUNY blurb).

Los Angeles, California
c. 1962

Teaches physiology and algebra for four years at Our Lady of Loretto High School, Los Angeles, where she meets and marries a fellow teacher. Four children follow (names not public).

Los Angeles, California
c. 1968

Earns a Montessori credential in London.

London
1969

Opens a Montessori school in Montana (one source: Kalispell). Over her career she teaches at pre-school, high-school and junior-college level.

Montana
