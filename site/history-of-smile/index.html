This page is the map of a series. Smile, the prompt language whose sections open and close with faces, grew over three years across ChatGPT conversations, Discord messages, Google documents, Claude conversations, two repositories and many Claude Code sessions. Here each of its ideas is dated to the first place it was spoken of, and placed inside the pillar it belongs to. Every idea below will become its own post, written from every word he said about it, in the order the ideas first appeared.

Every moment, on one line

Each dot is one moment, one idea spoken of in one conversation, document or recording. The lanes are the threads of the pillars, coloured by their region. A ringed dot is the first time an idea was said. The lower lanes are the days of commits and the days new project folders began. Hover or tap a dot to read the words, and press it to open that idea below.

How Smile grew

2023, the first prompts in ChatGPT

Smile begins as one request inside a ChatGPT conversation called My Persistent Prompt, on 2 August 2023. The third step of that prompt reads “3. Request GPT-4 to regenerate from NOISY representation into context window limit of the GPT-4 model a prompt composed of alphanumeric characters and emojis that will result in regeneration of”.1 A week later, on 9 August, he described the editor, an app that changes the interface you see while you write a prompt.2

On 8 September 2023 the delimiter arrived, in a conversation about a GPT-4 web app, with the words “revise stucture to be emojified instead, as these are superior to use in Large language Models as they gain a lot of attention”.3 The faces that later open and close every Smile section begin here, as emoji chosen for the attention a model gives them.

"The game of prompting" where first you learn the fundamentals: You're writing plain text into something that generates context, and what you write is context inside o…”.6

Questions

When was the Smile Prompt Language created?

His first words toward it are in a ChatGPT conversation of 2 August 2023, asking for a prompt made of letters and emojis. He shared the first version on Discord on 3 July 2025, and the public repository on GitHub began on 11 July 2025.

What is Smile?

Smile is a prompt language built from nested emoticons. The faces are the delimiters that open and close each section, and their features are the syntax.

How many ideas make up Smile?

55 sections of THE MAP, gathered into 12 threads inside 5 regions. Each section will be the subject of its own post.

From 3611 quotes of his across 1641 moments, each found character for character in its conversation, document or recording, and from 1341 commits to the public repository and 241 to the repository on his PC.
