# The New Body: EDS Insight with Ananda Purebuddha

Made from the DrThomasAger template. `site.toml` is the whole site:
its name, address, categories, colours, mailing list, shop, freebie and
words. Posts are markdown in `posts/`.

- Build: `python build.py <this folder>` from the engine's repository,
  or `python build.py` once this site is its own repository.
- The built site is `site/`. Open `site/index.html` to see it.
- Mailing list: `mailing/README.md`.
- The freebie, The Ultimate Supplement Stack: `freebie/the-ultimate-supplement-stack.md`.
