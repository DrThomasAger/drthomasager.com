Connective tissue is the body's glue: it holds joints, skin, vessels and organs in their places. In Ehlers-Danlos syndrome that glue is made differently, so it stretches more and holds less.

## How it shows up

Joints that bend further than most, that slip or dislocate. Skin that is soft, stretchy or bruises easily. Fatigue, pain, and a nervous system that is often busy.

## What helps

Knowing is the first help. Strength that protects the joints, pacing, and care for the whole body follow.
