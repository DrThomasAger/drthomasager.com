Mast cells are guardians in the tissues. They release histamine and many other messengers when they sense danger. In MCAS they release them too easily, and the whole body feels it: flushing, hives, stomach trouble, a racing heart, brain fog.

## EDS and MCAS together

The two are often found together, alongside POTS. Learning one often opens the door to understanding the others.
