The body we can touch and the body we feel from within are one body seen two ways. When the physical body is unwell, the spiritual body is still whole, and resting in it can bring ease to the other.

## A first practice

Sit or lie however is kind to your joints. Feel the breath arrive. Let attention rest in the heart, and let the body be exactly as it is.
