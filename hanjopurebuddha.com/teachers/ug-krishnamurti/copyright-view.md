# U.G. Krishnamurti on the copyright of his own words

## The statement

> My teaching, if that is the word you want to use, has no copyright. You
> are free to reproduce, distribute, interpret, misinterpret, distort,
> garble, do what you like, even claim authorship, without my consent or
> the permission of anybody.
>
> — U.G. Krishnamurti

Confirmed verbatim in this session by exact-phrase web search against
quotefancy, quotepark, the Penguin India listing of *The Penguin U.G.
Krishnamurti Reader* (ed. Mukunda Rao), the Wikisource edition of *Mind is
a Myth*, and the McMaster/WELL essay "The Unrational Philosophy of U.G.
Krishnamurti" (J.S.R.L. Narayana Moorty), which reproduces the second
sentence and remarks that it doubts this has any precedent in history.

## Where it is printed

The statement sits in the front matter, in place of a copyright notice, in
the books assembled from his recorded conversations.

Wikiquote files the statement under *The Mystique of Enlightenment* (1982)
with the note that it is a copyright release found in this and several
other publications of his conversations. Wikisource quotes it from *Mind is
a Myth*. The wording is identical in both.

## What he said about publishing at all

From the publisher's note to *Mind is a Myth* (Dinesh Vaghela, Dinesh
Publications, 1988), confirmed by search in this session:

> "Why bother publishing my conversations. It has not helped you, and it
> is not going to help anybody else", said U.G. when I approached him with
> the idea of publishing excerpts from his conversations.

The publisher adds that he went ahead anyway and published the first book
some years before. That first book was *The Mystique of Enlightenment*,
which U.G. called the "Mistake of Enlightenment".

## How the statement has been treated since

- **Wikisource** hosts *Mind is a Myth*, *Thought is Your Enemy*,
  *Courage to Stand Alone*, *No Way Out*, *The Natural State* and *The
  Mystique of Enlightenment* under the notice that the work is in the
  public domain worldwide because it has been so released by the copyright
  holder. The author page says some or all works by this author are in the
  public domain worldwide because they have been so released by the
  copyright holder, and that other works may be copyrighted.
- **Internet Archive** carries scans of *No Way Out*, *The Mystique of
  Enlightenment*, *The Natural State*, *The Courage to Stand Alone*,
  *Thought is Your Enemy*, *Mind is a Myth*, *The Book of Passages*, *A
  Life* and *On U.G.* under Public Domain Mark 1.0.
- **The WELL** (people.well.com/user/jct, maintained by J.S.R.L. Narayana
  Moorty and friends) placed all the works in their entirety for free
  download, citing the statement.
- **Commercial editions** continue to be sold (Sentient Publications 2007,
  Penguin India 2007, e-artnow collected editions 2021 and 2022,
  HarperCollins *UG Says* 2018). The statement permits that too. Nothing in
  it reserves anything for anyone.

## Material around the statement, for reading his view whole

- He repeated that he had no message for mankind. Mahesh Bhatt's biography
  says he endlessly repeated "I have no message for mankind."
- He founded no organisation, gave no lectures, held no gatherings, and
  asked that no rituals be performed at his death (older Wikipedia text,
  via the en-academic mirror). The same text says that as far as can be
  ascertained he never made a will and left no material property.
- The statement extends to authorship itself. The permission to "even
  claim authorship" removes the attribution interest, which is the one
  right most free licences keep.
- The statement is not a formal public-domain dedication in any legal
  template. It is a sentence in his own voice placed where a copyright
  line goes. Archives have treated it as a release by the copyright
  holder, and no estate has ever contested that, since there is no estate.
