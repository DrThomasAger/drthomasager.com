## 1. No Way Out (1991; Quotepark cites a 2002 edition with chapter titles)

1. "The fact is that we don't want to be free. What is responsible for our problems is the fear of losing what we have and what we know." — Goodreads work quotes 6212984
2. "Fear is the very thing that you do not want to be free from." — Goodreads 6212984; praveennv.blogspot 2007/09
3. "What you call 'yourself' is fear. The 'you' is born out of fear; it lives in fear, functions in fear and dies in fear." — Goodreads author page 30268 (p.2); praveennv.blogspot
4. "The body cannot be afraid of death. The movement that is created by society or culture is what does not want to come to an end." — Goodreads / praveennv.blogspot 2007/09
5. "What you are afraid of is not death." — Goodreads (same passage as #4)
6. "God or Enlightenment is the ultimate pleasure, uninterrupted happiness. No such thing exists." — Goodreads 6212984; praveennv.blogspot 2016/03 ("God or Enlightenment" post)
7. "It is fear that makes you believe that you are ..." (partial) — Goodreads quote 531154
8. "You love fear. The ending of fear is death, and you don't want that to happen. I am not talking of wiping out the..." (partial) — azquotes 755135; quotefancy 923772 (book not stated by site; themed with the NWO fear passages)
9. **[paraphrase]** "the death of fear is the only death" (summary wording of the continuation of #8) — quotefancy 923772
10. **[paraphrase]** death described as atoms changing form, with nothing born and nothing dead — Goodreads author page
11. Ch. 7 "What Kind Of Human Being Do You Want?" — Quotepark labels an NWO passage with this chapter; a passage "that ends with the speaker saying there is no escape" **[paraphrase]** — quotepark.com/authors/ug-krishnamurti
12. "ants-and-flies" passage — attributed to No Way Out on Goodreads and Quotepark (text not surfaced; **[paraphrase]**)
13. Goodreads work page 6212984 lists 12 quotes total ("No Way Out Quotes Showing 1-12 of 12"); Wikiquote notes "different editions of this work arrange and title the material differently."
14. Full text chapters exist on Wikisource: en.wikisource.org/wiki/No_Way_Out/Chapter_1 and /Chapter_6 (not fetched).

## 2. The Courage to Stand Alone (1997/2001, ed. Ellen J. Chrystal; "transcribed and edited from the famous 'Give Up' tapes recorded in Amsterdam in 1982"; Non-Duality Press edition 2013)

15. "You can have the courage to climb the mountain, swim the lakes, go on a raft to the other side of the Atlantic or Pacific. That is not courage. ..." (partial) — Goodreads quote 590899; quotepark 1922951; summary continuation: "the courage to stand on your own two feet cannot be given by somebody" **[paraphrase of ending]**; another summary gives the phrase "stand on your two solid feet" as in-quote wording.
16. "Courage is not an instrument or quality you can use to get somewhere. The stopping of doing is courage." — Goodreads 6212989 (the second sentence also appears on Mind Is a Myth collections)
17. "Nature is busy creating absolutely unique individuals, whereas culture has invented a single mold to which all must conform." — Goodreads 6212989; azquotes culture tag; praveennv 2016/03 "Each individual is unique"
18. **[paraphrase]** "each time a thought is born, you are born" and "thought is shortlived" — reading traditional rebirth as this cycle — Goodreads 6212989
19. "it has to dawn on you that it is not leading you anywhere" (partial) — Wikiquote section "The Courage to Stand Alone: Conversations with U.G. Krishnamurti (2001)"
20. "There seems to be no way out." — closing line of a CTSA passage quoted on praveennv.blogspot (blog tagline: "There seems to be no way out" Ug Krishnamurti)
21. **[paraphrase]** "you cannot look at your thought, because you cannot separate yourself from thought" — praveennv.blogspot (excerpt from CTSA)
22. **[paraphrase]** "Questioning the solutions requires a tremendous courage" — praveennv.blogspot 2016/03 post title quoting CTSA
23. Goodreads notes the "entire burden of the past" line "appears in slightly different forms on different pages" (text not surfaced).
24. Goodreads 6212989 holds nine quotes; Wikisource has the full text (en.wikisource.org/wiki/Courage_to_Stand_Alone); Part III at beyond-the-pale.co.uk/ugk3.htm; PDF at o-meditation.com.

## 6. My Swan Song (dictated to Louis Brawley, mid-February 2007, Vallecrosia; UG died 22 March 2007) — partial recovery

Sources: people.well.com/user/jct/SWAN_SONG.htm (WELL transcription; page title in caps), ugkrishnamurti.in/ug-essence/my-swan-song, medium.com/@alysion42 ("[Updated]" version, differs in wording and contains an inserted "Eric Lee" commentary section), groups.google.com/g/noshonuts/c/fGiwo09RzyI, sciforums thread 95095, YouTube Sj57odcs1dI (audio), remembering-ug.blogspot 2012/08 "Video - Swan Song".

53. WELL page title (all caps as published): "WHAT I HAVE FOUND, OFF AND BY MYSELF, IS THAT ALL TEACHERS RELIGIOUS, SECULAR AND THOSE SCIENTISTS AND FILTHY DOCTORS ARE WRON[G]" (truncated in listing)
54. Opening line (ugkrishnamurti.in): "What I have found of and by myself," ... **[paraphrase of continuation]**: his findings "run counter to everything anyone has said in any field of human thought" (Google Groups / sciforums copies)
55. **[paraphrase]** the human being is "more despicable than all other life forms on the planet" (Google Groups copy)
56. **[paraphrase]** "there is no need to change this world or yourself" (ugkrishnamurti.in copy)
57. "Although I assert that all doctors should be shot, I don't advise others not to see a doctor." — Medium/ugkrishnamurti.in (ugkrishnamurti.in summary renders it "all doctors should be shot, I don't advise others not to see a doctor")
58. "I don't know what I will do if I am in a situation where I want to prolong my life a little longer." — Medium (alysion42)
59. **[paraphrase]** he "would never tell others not to see a doctor" — ugkrishnamurti.in
60. **[paraphrase]** freeing the body "from what is put in it by spiritual teachers or secular teachers, or by those scientists and medical technology" (wording differs across copies)
61. **[paraphrase]** sex "is for reproduction"; people "have turned it into a pleasure movement"; "an enlightened man can never have sex because he cannot reproduce another one like him" — ugkrishnamurti.in
62. **[paraphrase]** "the native intelligence of the human body is amazing" and it is "all it needs to survive in any dangerous situation in life" — ugkrishnamurti.in
63. "a clinical death will occur, and it is not the near-death experience of those 'near death' scoundrels" — WELL / Google Groups (one copy; summary says another copy "reproduces the same remark in full")
64. **[paraphrase]** his experience "has nothing to do with the spiritual nonsense they preach"
65. "you keep buying these bogus wares offered up by the experts" — one transcription (WELL or Medium)
66. religious and political figures are "frauds who promise you some goodies that they can never deliver" — Swan Song copies
67. Editorial note (ug-krishnamurti.blogspot 2010/09, "First and last meetings with UG"): the Swan Song was "earlier dictated to Louis which was now only on Louis's computer"; the blogger says he edited it because "the English needed work and the piece wasn't all that coherent" — so circulating versions differ.
68. Wikipedia: "In February 2007, he had dictated his final speech, 'My Swan Song'." He "refused medical or other external intervention and decided to let his body take its own natural course" after a fall.

Not confirmed in Swan Song by any result: "I don't give a damn", "mahatmas", "love", "mystique", "Theosophical Society".

## Attribution conflicts

- "You are what you do, not what you say you want to do": Mahesh Bhatt's own words in A Taste of Death, often misattributed to UG (Wikiquote).
- jhana8.com/u-g-krishnamurti and azquotes mix in quotes "actually attributed to his better-known namesake, Jiddu Krishnamurti" (search summary). Treat any azquotes item not corroborated elsewhere as suspect.
- Wikiquote Talk page flags "a misprinted quote from Mind Is a Myth" and "some unsourced quotes" on the main page.
- "The stopping of doing is courage" is attributed both to Courage to Stand Alone (Goodreads 6212989) and to Mind Is a Myth collections.
- "Nature is busy creating absolutely unique individuals..." attributed to CTSA (Goodreads) and used untagged on azquotes.
- "You love fear. The ending of fear is death..." (azquotes/quotefancy) is not assigned to a book by those sites; thematically matches No Way Out.
- "man/mind has created God out of fear": praveennv cites Mind Is a Myth p.31 ("it is the mind that..."); azquotes renders "man has created God out of fear".
- Swan Song: the Medium "[Updated]" text differs from WELL/ugkrishnamurti.in and contains inserted third-party commentary ("Eric Lee" section); a blogger admits editing the dictation for coherence.
- Wikiquote dates The Courage to Stand Alone 2001 (Chrystal edition), the task brief says 1997; Quotepark dates No Way Out 2002 (reprint) vs 1991 original.
- UG's age at death: sources give 88 vs 89.
