# U.G. Krishnamurti (Uppaluri Gopala Krishnamurti, 1918–2007) — Catalogue of Image Sources

**Scope note:** Jiddu Krishnamurti results were discarded throughout (e.g. kfoundation.org photo pages, Commons `File:Jiddu_Krishnamurti_01.jpg`, `File:Bourdelle-JidduKrishnamurti.jpg`, Getty/Alamy "Krishnamurti" sets, Pinterest "Jiddu Krishnamurti Photos"). Where a mixed source contains both men it is flagged.

**Tool note:** 26 WebSearch calls were completed before the turn's shared search budget (200 calls across all agents) ran out; 16 further planned queries were refused and are listed in the final section. WebFetch/curl were unavailable, so page contents are described from search snippets only — images were not directly viewed, and licences are stated only where the snippet gave them.
