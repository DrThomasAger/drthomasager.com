## 3. Places
- **Chalet Sunbeam, Gstaad** — "Valentine on the terrace of chalet Sunbeam, Gstaad" caption in the illustrated PDF *Remembrance – UG and Valentine* (https://bijouti-tech.com/REMEMBRANCE.pdf). Context: two-storey chalet on a small hill opposite the Gstaad tennis courts, landlord Herr Grossman (Moorty blog, "Notable Travels"); Friedrich Grohe newsletter Fall 1993 places gatherings on the lawn overlooking the courts. [S]
- **Chalet Pfynegg** — text only (WELL ugbio8: "the chalet behind the bench"); no image.
- **Gstaad 1992** — YouTube "U.G. Krishnamurti with friends – Gstaad 1992" (edit Enzo de Ruvo) for stills. [lead]
- **Yercaud** — archive.org `ug_yercaud95` (1995 video) is the only imagery; the "colonial house" (20×70 ft on a 100×100 ft plot) described in *Stopped in Our Tracks*. [lead]
- **Poornakutee, Basavanagudi** — text only: originally "Sastri Sadana", three-storey house facing the Anjaneya Temple (Chandrasekhar). No photo.
- **Hridaya Vihar** — Chandrasekhar's house, 345 3rd Main Rd, Block 2, Banashankari 3rd Stage, Bengaluru 560085 (Mappls listing); no photo.
- **Vallecrosia villa** (apartment built by Lucia, Anita and Giovanni) — text only; no photo.
- **Palm Springs / Mill Valley / Seaside / Carmel** — no house photographs; Carmel rental (one month) described on Moorty blog; WELL ug_1 Carmel caption only.
