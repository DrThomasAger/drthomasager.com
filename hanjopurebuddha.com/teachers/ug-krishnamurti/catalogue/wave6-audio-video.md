# U.G. Krishnamurti — Audio/Video catalogue, wave 6

Scope: Uppaluri Gopala Krishnamurti (1918–2007) only. Jiddu Krishnamurti material excluded. Research date 2026-10-24, ~51 web searches (WebSearch only; no page fetches, so playlist/channel contents could not be enumerated directly). Items already catalogued in earlier waves are skipped except where a new ID or fact attaches to them. Machine-readable version: `scratchpad/json/wave6-av.json` (87 items; 70 S, 17 lead).

Confidence: **S** = title/ID/date seen in a search result; **lead** = inferred or partially seen, needs verification.

## 6. Tape formats and custodians

- **Julie Thayer**: began filming in 1989 with a *rented* video camera (travelswithug.com Journal One); c. 100 hours unedited tapes per the Schonewille-era source, "hundreds of hours" 1989–2007 per 2026 reviews; footage "unpolished and degraded". Dougherty found footage and journals online c. 2008, Thayer gave permission; film 18 years in the making; CID fiscal sponsorship listing "The UG Krishnamurti Documentary". **Tape format (Hi8/VHS/Video8) and who digitised: not found.**
- **Mahesh Bhatt**: no record of his own masters; he assisted Dougherty and is EP. Archive.org `ug_ug_w_mb_150` source not stated.
- **Terry Newland, Rodney Arms, Noronha**: appear only as book editors; no tape-custody record found. remembering-ug "Give Up & Terry Newland" post pairs Newland with the Give Up audio.
- **Valentine de Kerven**: no tape record found.
- **Chandrasekhar**: *Stopped in Our Tracks* states UG's collected files, photos, video and audio tapes were moved to Yercaud at his insistence; Book Two compiled from "various audio tapes"; his YouTube channel `@sekharsug63` (via ugkrishnamurti.in) and archive.org `ug_yercaud95` are the visible outputs (S).
- **Henk Schonewille**: ~24 hours of Amsterdam talks recorded with permission (audio, not video); he produced/edited the original tapes; transcribed by Ellen Chrystal → *The Courage to Stand Alone* (Amsterdam, Sept 1982); "Give Up" = 3 cassettes, 4.5 h; YouTube `8Vau7s1cuCM` is from these tapes (S).
- **Bohm 1968-08-17**: archive.org `ug_ug_bohm`; Audiomack; YouTube `tYI8BIky9H4`, `pmi_u9VNFDE`; David Bohm Society lists "David Bohm in Conversation with U. G. Krishnamurti (1968) [Free]" and notes the Krishnamurti Archive holds some Bohm recordings; Saanen location not confirmed (S).
- **thisrobotdreaming**: blog post "U. G. Krishnamurti tapes" (c. May 2025) — author uploaded all their UG cassette tapes, 1967–1990s, converted to MP3, to archive.org. Specific identifiers not exposed; candidates `ug_there_is_no_such_thing_as_ultimate_reality`, `ug_what_is_going_on_here_is_sa`, `ug_ug_with_unknown_individual` (lead).
- **NIMHANS 1976**: ugkrishnamurti.in says recorded sessions with NIMHANS doctors (1976) are available in video with PDFs (lead).
