## 6. Carr, Guha, Bansal, editors

- **Robert (Bob) Carr** (b. 1928; artist, magician, TV producer; met UG 1966): *God Men Con Men*, Smriti Books 2003 with audio disc "Conversations with U.G. Krishnamurti" (OCLC 56594816); later *God Men Con Men: Pursuit of Truth*, ISBN 9780990585046; introduction to *The End of All Beginnings* (2014, ISBN 099058500X). No separate film by Carr confirmed.
- **Sabyasachi Guha**: *In Palm Springs with U.G. Krishnamurti* (Motilal Banarsidass 2021; HB 9789391024239, PB 9789391024314) = the circulating "14 Days in Palm Springs with UG Krishnamurti" (preface Oct 2007; Guha sole author, not Lakshmi); *Back to Square One* (Parting Shot Press 2015, ISBN 9781936940899); *Life Finds Its Way* (ed. Nandini Kapadia, Zen Publications 2019, ISBN 9789387242357); *Guha Talks with the Mother of God* (Epigraph Books 2012); Bengali translation of Bhatt biography (2011).
- **Sunita Pant Bansal**, *The Ashes Are Warm: Memories of a Lifetime Spent with UG Krishnamurti* (Mahesh Bhatt as told to SPB), Rupa Publications, 5 April 2026 (Amazon 30 April), 384 pp., ISBN 978-93-7646-180-6, Rs 495; Kindle B0GX2WTJ87; excerpts Scroll 17 May 2026, ThePrint; review FPJ July 2026.
- **Editors**: Terry Newland (Mind Is a Myth 1987/88; Smriti 2003 with Bansal), Ellen J. Chrystal (Courage to Stand Alone), Rodney Arms (Mystique of Enlightenment), Peter Maverick (The Natural State, 2005). No memoirs/articles by them beyond introductions surfaced.
- **Louis Brawley**, *Goner* rev. ed. ISBN 9780956643278. **Julie Thayer**, *Get Lost and Stay Lost* (unofficial upload only).

## 7. Theosophical-era and 1960s primary documents

- Secondary timeline (ugkrishnamurti.in key-events; ugarchive.blogspot; encyclopedia.com): Dec 1932 convention, Jinarajadasa gives him *I Promise*; c.1939–41 Leadbeater library, TS lecturer; Joint General Secretary of Indian Section, National Lecturer 7 years, Europe tours; 1949 first New York visit as international lecturer; July 1953 break with TS/Jinarajadasa.
- **Primary letters**: 30 Dec 1961 letter to his wife (ends marriage) and 7 Sep 1963 letter to a swami at the Ramakrishna Vedanta Centre, London — quoted in *UG: A Life* ch. 5 (`ugbio/ugbio5.htm`; mirror ug-k.blogspot.com). The "1961 letter to the London Ramakrishna Mission" in the brief appears to be this cluster.
- *The Theosophist* scans on iapsop.com (e.g. v69 n9, June 1948, ed. Jinarajadasa) — no UG item confirmed; lead only. No 1949 New York lecture text found.
