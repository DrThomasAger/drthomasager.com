# U.G. Krishnamurti — Wave 4 dated chronology (1918–2007, with posthumous publications)

Scope: Uppaluri Gopala Krishnamurti only (not Jiddu Krishnamurti). Built from ~50 web searches (WebFetch unavailable), drawing on Mahesh Bhatt's *A Life* (WELL chapters), K. Chandrasekhar's *Stopped in Our Tracks* (Wikisource/WELL/blog reprints), Shanta Kelker's *The Sage and the Housewife*, Narayana Moorty's blog, Julie Thayer's *Travels with UG*, Louis Brawley's *Goner*, Mark Whitwell's essays, the WELL streaming index, archive.org item dates, ugkrishnamurti.in key-events, Wikipedia, encyclopedia.com and press.

Confidence: **S** = stated in a primary/close source and uncontradicted; **T** = tentative (single or secondary source, or inferred year); **conflict** = sources disagree (see section below).

JSON twin: `scratchpad/json/events.json` (84 events).

## Conflicting dates

- **First Theosophical encounter with Besant/Jinarajadasa** — Bhatt (*A Life* ch. 2): Golden Jubilee convention, December 1925, Adyar. Official key-events: December 1932 convention, when Jinarajadasa gave him *I Promise*. Possibly two distinct events.
- **University of Madras degree** — encyclopedia.com: bachelor's degrees in philosophy and psychology. Wikipedia/Bhatt: left without completing a degree after his grandfather died; UG himself said he was 'not interested in a degree'.
- **Joint Secretary of the Indian Section** — 1946 (Penguin Reader, after Jinarajadasa's election) vs 1947 (other summaries). Lecturing period 'about seven years', ending 1953.
- **Number of children** — Three (Bhatt ch. 4: three, then a fourth boy born in the USA) vs four; obituary names Usha, Bharati and Kumar; WELL photos name Bharati, Vasant and Usha. Vasant died 1982.
- **End of the US lecturing period** — Official table 1955–1959; other accounts say he stopped after about two years; departure for London universally 1961 (official table frames Europe as 1960–1963).
- **Kusuma Kumari's death (1963)** — Cause/place: died of an accident after shock therapy (Wikipedia mirror) vs 'died in a mental institution' (encyclopedia.com) vs 'soon after a plea for death' (Chandrasekhar). Year 1963 consistent.
- **Meeting Valentine de Kerven** — December 1963 (official timeline, 'after Christmas') vs 1964 (second-hand gossip quoted in *Stopped in Our Tracks*).
- **Onset/location of the 1967 'calamity'** — Saanen, week ending 9 July 1967 (Wikipedia, Bhatt, Chandrasekhar). Official life-background page: experiences begin in Paris, April 1967. Mukunda Rao's *Biology of Enlightenment* intro says JK came to Saanen in August 1967.
- **First public talk, IIWC Bangalore** — 1972 (encyclopedia.com, official table) vs May 1972 (Chandrasekhar). Arrival in Bangalore January 1972.
- **Bhatt/Parveen Babi visit, 1979** — Bhatt: September 1979, Kodaikanal. Chandrasekhar: arrived Bangalore 3 October 1979, then Mysore, later Kodaikanal.
- ***Mind is a Myth* first edition** — 1987, Crest Associates, Vallabhvidyanagar (PhilPapers, Wikiquote) vs 1988 (several lists); a Dinesh/Goa edition unconfirmed. Intro signed Mill Valley, December 1985.
- ***The Courage to Stand Alone*** — 2001, Smriti Books (Wikiquote) vs 'first published 1997' (Goodreads).
- **Valentine de Kerven's death** — Consistently 20 January 1991, Bangalore (Chandrasekhar; official site). Earlier guesses of 1999/2001 are unsupported.
- **Death of UG** — 22 March 2007 (all sources; one WELL memorial page says 25 March). Time 2:30 p.m. (official obituary) vs 2:30 a.m. (some reports). Age 88 (Wikipedia) vs 89 (WELL). Cremation 23 March 2:45 p.m.
- ***The Natural State* documentary premiere** — Telluride, 7 September 2026 (Tribune) vs Saturday 5 September 2026 (Deadline preview).

## Gaps not resolved by search

- Exact dates of the 1940s–50s Theosophical lecture tours in Europe (Norway etc.) and the 1949 New York visit beyond the official table's year.
- Precise dates of Bombay visits 1968–71, the first TV interview (1986) and Doordarshan/BBC interview dates.
- Year-by-year itinerary 1999–2006 (Palm Springs winters, Vallecrosia house completion, German car trips) beyond what Brawley/Thayer/Moorty excerpts give; the *Goner* chapter headings ('India 2004–2005', '14 days in Palm Springs') are the only anchors found.
