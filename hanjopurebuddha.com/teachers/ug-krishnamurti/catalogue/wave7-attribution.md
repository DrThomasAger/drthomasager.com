## Notes

- qdb3fcee7 is NOT U.G.'s own sentence: it is from Dr. T.R. Raghunath's introductory essay (No Way Out Ch. 1).
- qc213105f appears in Terry Newland's editorial Introduction to Mind is a Myth as a sampler of U.G.'s claims.
- q45036753 and q7660d023 are U.G.'s words as quoted inside Mahesh Bhatt's biography; qcc769ade, q46e8ac7b, q6fe42d31 are from Bhatt's diary 'A Taste of Death' (well.com mahesh.html, 'Snatches from U.G.'s Conversations').
- q39067cd0, q5e5b2970, q00193fb7 live in K. Chandrasekhar's 'Excerpts from U.G.'s Dialogues, compiled from audio tapes' (well.com chandra.htm), which is not one of the published books; recorded as 'interview: K. Chandrasekhar audio-tape excerpts'.
- The well.com page Final_Remembering.htm is a memorial quote compilation; eight unassigned quotes are attested only there (and on inner-quest.org), so they stay unresolved.
- Several quotes (qc032db3b, q74cba27f, q9a924078, qea825286) trace only to Goodreads work 43411143, a self-published 'U.G. Krishnamurti Quotes' booklet, not to a canonical title.
