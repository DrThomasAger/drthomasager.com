# Wave 5 — ugkrishnamurti.in site inventory (U.G. Krishnamurti only)

Date: 2026-10-24. Scope: Uppaluri Gopala Krishnamurti (1918–2007); Jiddu Krishnamurti excluded.

**Method note.** Two passes. Pass 1 (search budget exhausted) consolidated every ugkrishnamurti.in / .org / .net record gathered by waves 1–4 (catalogue files, items.json, quotes.json, download-manifest.json, scratchpad JSON). Pass 2 ran 42 extended web searches (`site:ugkrishnamurti.in` is only loosely honoured by the engine, so most hits came from distinctive-title and path queries); WebFetch was unavailable, so everything rests on search-result snippets of the live pages. Confidence **S** = the item/title/label was observed in a snippet of the ugkrishnamurti.in page itself; **lead** = inferred from a navigation label, a single uncorroborated mention, or a pattern-guessed URL. Durations are not shown on the site; the two given come from the older 'Essential UG' archive (ugkrishnamurti.org) for the same recordings.

**What pass 2 changed.** Books & PDFs: full nine-title 'Read Now' list confirmed (adds *The Sage and the Housewife*; all six conversation-book PDFs upgraded lead → S). Remembering UG: sub-section 'Reflections by Friends', first contributor Frank Noronha. UG Speaks: sub-section names confirmed (Essential Audios / Essential Videos / Talks with NIMHANS Doctors / Interviews with UG); Interviews page dated '1981 - 1992' with labels AMSTERDAM_19820901, AMSTERDAM TALKS 1982 (new), AMSTERDAM_19920909; Essential Audios labels incl. SUSANDEAN KHFM ALBUQUERQUE (19 Apr 1991, 32 min), UG DONNALDINGRAM (7 Mar 1990, 29 min), ROBERT G F AMSTERDAM 1987 (new), SYDNEY1994, WILLEM DE RIDDER AMSTERDAM; Old Audios: 'UG with Bob Carr and others in Bangalore' heading, three SC Birla Bangalore sessions 12/14 Mar 1995 (new), exact labels for the Amsterdam 1984/1987 items. - **About UG / Life Background (biography; obituary account by Mukunda Rao)** — Page title 'UGK - Life Background'. Biographical page: born 1918 (spelled 'Masulipatam') into a Telugu Brahmin family, raised in a religious household by a Theosophist grandfather; University of Madras studies 'gave him no answers'; met Ramana Maharshi at 21; married 1943, four children; died March 2007 in Italy. Includes Mukunda Rao's obituary account: UG fell and injured himself about seven weeks before his death, refused medical treatment, died 22 March 2007 at 2:30 pm aged 88/89 in the apartment built for him by friends Lucia, Anita and Giovanni in their villa at Vallecrosia, Italy; cremated next day (23 March, 2:45 pm) by Mahesh Bhatt without rituals. Page carries photographs.
- **Life & Background** — Life-background sub-page. Gives birth at Masulipatnam 9 July 1918; the December 1953 encounter with J. Krishnamurti; and places the onset of 'the most astounding and almost incomprehensible mystical experiences' in Paris, April 1967, in his 49th year (only this site gives Paris/April).
- **Valentine de Kerven (page)** — Page on Valentine de Kerven, the Swiss consulate translator who sheltered UG in Geneva in December 1963 (she was about 63), gave up her job to look after him, set him up in Saanen near Gstaad during the 'halcyon days' 1963-67, was moved permanently to Bangalore in September 1986 as Alzheimer's advanced, and died at Poornakutee, Bangalore, 20 January 1991.
- **Key Events (timeline page)** — Dated timeline of UG's life, presented as a table ('official key-events table'). Individual dated entries are itemised below. Page carries photographs.
