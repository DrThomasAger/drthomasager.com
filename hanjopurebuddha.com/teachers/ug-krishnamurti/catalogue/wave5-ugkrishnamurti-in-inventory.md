# Wave 5 — ugkrishnamurti.in site inventory (U.G. Krishnamurti only)

Date: 2026-10-24. Scope: Uppaluri Gopala Krishnamurti (1918–2007); Jiddu Krishnamurti excluded.

**Method note.** The shared per-turn web-search budget was already exhausted when this wave started, so no new searches could be run. This inventory consolidates every ugkrishnamurti.in / .org / .net record gathered by waves 1–4 (catalogue files, items.json, quotes.json, download-manifest.json, scratchpad JSON), de-duplicated and re-keyed per section. Confidence **S** = the item/title was observed in a search result or page snippet in an earlier wave; **lead** = inferred from navigation labels, the task brief, or a single uncorroborated mention (URL may be guessed). Durations were never captured for any recording. Items not recovered at all: individual Explore Videos titles; individual Remembering UG stories/authors; the full press-clipping list; the exact PDF link targets; gallery URL.

Total items: 105

- **About UG (biography / obituary account by Mukunda Rao)** — Biographical page. Includes Mukunda Rao's obituary account: UG fell and injured himself about seven weeks before his death, refused medical treatment, died 22 March 2007 at 2:30 pm aged 88/89 in the apartment built for him by friends Lucia, Anita and Giovanni in their villa at Vallecrosia, Italy; cremated next day (23 March, 2:45 pm) by Mahesh Bhatt without rituals. Page carries photographs.
- **Life & Background** — Life-background sub-page. Gives birth at Masulipatnam 9 July 1918; the December 1953 encounter with J. Krishnamurti; and places the onset of 'the most astounding and almost incomprehensible mystical experiences' in Paris, April 1967, in his 49th year (only this site gives Paris/April).
- **Valentine de Kerven (page)** — Page on Valentine de Kerven, the Swiss consulate translator who sheltered UG in Geneva in December 1963 (she was about 63), gave up her job to look after him, set him up in Saanen near Gstaad during the 'halcyon days' 1963-67, was moved permanently to Bangalore in September 1986 as Alzheimer's advanced, and died at Poornakutee, Bangalore, 20 January 1991.
- **Key Events (timeline page)** — Dated timeline of UG's life, presented as a table ('official key-events table'). Individual dated entries are itemised below. Page carries photographs.
