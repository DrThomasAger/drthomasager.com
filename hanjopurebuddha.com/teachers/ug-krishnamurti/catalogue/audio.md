# Catalogue of AUDIO recordings of U.G. Krishnamurti (Uppaluri Gopala Krishnamurti, 1918–2007)

**Scope note.** Only U.G. Krishnamurti. Every J. Krishnamurti hit that search engines returned (Krishnamurti Foundation Trust podcasts, KFA archives, "Urgency of Change", Donald Ingram Smith/J.K. Ojai 1977–80 conversations, Saanen 1984 Q&A directories, krishnamurtisubtitles.info, TKBAK directory, etc.) was discarded.

**Tool/coverage note.** Only WebSearch was available. 26 searches succeeded before the per-turn web-search budget (shared across all agents) was exhausted; a further 16 planned searches (Telugu/Kannada/Hindi-script queries, Spotify episode list, ElevenReader, Willem de Ridder radio, Biology-of-Enlightenment tape provenance, Chandrasekhar video tapes, Peter Maverick "Natural State", Sat Sangha Salon list, Conscious TV/Thinking Allowed, German/Italian audio, YouTube audiobook readings, archive.org identifier sweeps) were refused. They are listed in the Gaps section so a follow-up message can continue the sweep. Entries marked **[bg]** come from general background knowledge of the books' prefaces and were NOT verified by a search in this run.

## 1. Original cassette / reel tapes and who made them (provenance of the books)

- **Henk Schonewille — Amsterdam, September 1982 ("Give Up" tapes).** Three weeks of talks in Amsterdam; Schonewille's tape recorder captured almost all of them, c. 24 hours of material, with U.G.'s permission. Released as the "Give Up" series of 3 cassettes, total c. 4½ hours (Schonewille's 1995 foreword). Transcribed and edited by Ellen J. Chrystal into *The Courage to Stand Alone* (later revised/expanded edition, New Harbinger, "transcribed and edited from the famous 'Give Up' tapes recorded in Amsterdam in 1982"). Dutch edition: *De moed om te zijn wie je bent* (states the three weeks produced almost 24 hours of recordings; UG did not attach importance to copyright, so many editions appeared, especially in India). Format: audio cassette (3-cassette commercial release).
  - https://archive.org/details/UGKrishnamurtiTheCourageToStandAlone
  - https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Courage_to_Stand_Alone
  - https://www.newharbinger.com/9781626256811/the-courage-to-stand-alone/
  - https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/100653389-de-moed-om-te-zijn-wie-je-bent
  - https://o-meditation.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/the-courage-to-stand-alone.pdf
  - Audio of "Give Up" online: https://soundcloud.com/petermaverick/give-up-u-g-krishnamurti ("Amsterdam 1982 (produced by Henk Schonewille)")
  - Blog post pairing Give Up with Terry Newland material: http://remembering-ug.blogspot.com/2012/08/talks-give-up-terry-newland.html
- **Terry Newland — *Mind Is a Myth: Disquieting Conversations with the Man Called U.G.* (Dinesh Publications, 1988; PhilPapers dates 1987).** Edited by Terry Newland. A blog account says Newland brought mimeographed copies of conversations with U.G.; **[bg]** the book's own introduction says it was compiled from tape-recorded conversations in Bangalore and elsewhere in the mid-1980s. Who holds the Newland tapes: not found.
  - https://philpapers.org/rec/KRIDCW
  - https://philpapers.org/rec/KRIMIA
  - http://ug-krishnamurti.blogspot.com/2010/09/first-and-last-meetings-with-ug-dr.html
  - Computer-voice audio version: https://archive.org/details/ug_mind_myth
- **Rodney Arms — *The Mystique of Enlightenment* (ed. Rodney Arms; later edition credits Rodney Arms & Sunita Pant Bansal).** **[bg]** Compiled from tapes of conversations recorded in Gstaad and Bangalore 1973–1980; no source found in this run on who physically recorded or where tapes are.
  - https://philpapers.org/rec/KRITBO-5 (also lists *The Biology of Enlightenment: unpublished conversations 1967–71*)
  - https://people.well.com/user/jct/lists.html (Books, Interviews, and References list)
- **J.S.R.L. Narayana Moorty & Antony Paul Frank Noronha — *Thought Is Your Enemy* (Sowmya Publishers, Bangalore, 1990; co-editor Sunita Pant Bansal) and *No Way Out* (2002, ed. Noronha & Bansal).** Wikisource: compiled from discussions with inquirers in India, Switzerland, Australia, Netherlands and UK between 1985 and 1990 (Amazon's blurb adds Austria). A memoir excerpt notes Narayana Moorty lent audio tapes to a visitor, i.e. Moorty held a personal tape collection. Noronha's tape archive: not located.
  - https://archive.org/details/thoughtisyourene0000unse
  - https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Thought_is_Your_Enemy
  - https://www.amazon.co.uk/Thought-Your-Enemy-Conversations-Krishnamurti/dp/8187967110
  - https://www.amazon.com/Thought-Your-Enemy-Conversations-Krishnamurthi/dp/8187967110
  - https://indiaclub.com/products/8990-thought-is-your-enemy-conversations-with-u-g-krishnamurti
  - https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/U._G._Krishnamurti (notes editions of *No Way Out* differ)
- **Narayana Moorty — *Stopped in Our Tracks* (memoir with transcripts, Moorty's own tapes):** https://archive.org/details/UGKrishnamurtiStoppedInOurTracks227p
- **1967–71 Gstaad tapes (*The Biology of Enlightenment*, ed. Mukunda Rao).** Earliest known recording online is a 9 Sept 1967 Gstaad conversation (see §2). **[bg]** Valentine de Kerven/early Swiss friends recorded these; not verified here.
  - https://archive.org/details/ug_1967-09-09_gstaad
- **Mahesh Bhatt — Bombay TV/video material (see §2, §4).** Bhatt's own recordings are the basis of his books (*U.G. Krishnamurti: A Life*; *A Taste of Life*, the latter a record of 30 days in Gstaad, 1995).
- **Julie Thayer** — *Get Lost and Stay Lost: Travels with UG Krishnamurti / UG Krishnamurti Cookbook* (photographer/videographer in UG's circle; her video archive underlies many YouTube clips). **[bg]** Not verified by audio-specific search.
  - https://dokumen.pub/get-lost-and-stay-lost-travels-with-ug-krishnamurti-ug-krishnamurti-cookbook-by-julie-thayer.html
- **Chandrasekhar / Bangalore friends ("Chandrashekar Babu", Dr K.B. Rao, Bramchari)** — appear as participants in an archive.org audio item sourced from ugkrishnamurti.org (see §2).
- **Louis Brawley** — took the "My Swan Song" dictation, Feb 2007 (see §7).
- **Recording failure anecdote:** one source says a friend tried to record UG and the tapes ran blank (http://ug-krishnamurti.blogspot.com/2010/09/first-and-last-meetings-with-ug-dr.html).
- **Collection-level note:** The Internet Archive audio collection description states UG wrote no books; he held informal discussions with small groups and *others* recorded his talks. Collection "created by Jeff Kaplan"; several items credit source http://ugkrishnamurti.org.
