# U.G. Krishnamurti — Wave 2: Images, Archives/Sites, Communities, Places

Second-wave web research (24 Oct 2026, WebSearch only; no page fetches). Scope: Uppaluri Gopala Krishnamurti (1918–2007) only; Jiddu Krishnamurti and the Krishnamurti Foundations excluded. 46 searches. Machine-readable twin: `scratchpad/json/wave2-img-arch.json` (55 items; 44 confirmed "S", 11 "lead").

Confidence key: **S** = URL and content confirmed by a search result; **lead** = exists/plausible but URL, content or status not confirmed.

### 1f. Photographers of UG
- **Julie Thayer** — states she took "well over 300 photographs" (*Travels with UG / Get Lost and Stay Lost*, http://www.travelswithug.com/ ; journal http://www.travelswithug.com/journal-one.php). No gallery URL.

(lead for UG photo set)

## 4. Places and memorials

- **Bangalore — UG Aradhana** (annual, 22 March, death anniversary) at "Hridaya Vihar", home of K. Chandrasekhar and Suguna: 4th meet 22 Mar 2011; 11th meet 22 Mar 2018 (with photos). https://sulochanosho.wordpress.com/2018/03/28/ug-aradhana-meet-11th/ · https://sulochanosho.wordpress.com/tag/ug-friends/ (S). No 9 July gathering found.
- **Machilipatnam / Gudivada** — birthplace (some sources give Gudivada); no memorial, plaque or house museum found. https://www.ugkrishnamurti.in/about-ug ; family tree https://www.imeuswe.in/famous-families/Uppaluri-Krishnamurti (lead)
- **Vallecrosia** — died 22 Mar 2007 at a friend's villa; cremated next day by Bhatt without rites (https://www.oneindia.com/2007/03/24/philosopher-ug-krishnamurti-passes-away.html). No plaque/targa or villa photo found (lead).
- **Gstaad — Chalet Sunbeam** — rented ~25 summers; two storeys, landlord Herr Grossman; lawn over the tennis courts; 1976 garden recollection; Bhatt *A Taste of Death* summer 1995. Sources: https://people.well.com/user/jct/SIT/part_ii.htm ; http://moortysblogpage.blogspot.com/2009/03/notable-travels-and-visits-times-spent.html ; http://ug-k.blogspot.com/2007/04/taste-of-death-gstaad-summer-1995-10.html ; https://bijouti-tech.com/REMEMBRANCE.pdf . No chalet photo found (lead).
- **Yercaud** — 1995 talk video `ug_yercaud95`; late-life stays in a remote house (lead for photos).
- **Poornakutee, Bangalore** — residence; Valentine died there (Stopped in Our Tracks). No photos found (lead).
- **UG trust/centre in India** — none found.
