## Attribution conflicts

1. **Cyclone / eye / stillpoint passage.** azquotes cites "When there is no longer a cyclone, there is no longer an eye." to *The Path to No-Self* p.47. Goodreads tags the full stillpoint passage (Christ and Buddha, eye of a cyclone, "refuge from the swirling cyclone of suffering") to *The Christian Contemplative Journey: Essays on the Path*, and the identical passage appears in the 1986 Yoga Journal interview by Stephan Bodian (spiritualteachers.org / awakeningtoreality.com reprints). The interview is the most likely origin; the Essays volume may reprint it.
2. **"As a Christian, I saw the no-self experience as the true nature of Christ's death..."** Goodreads: *The Christian Contemplative Journey*. One search summary: *The Experience of No-Self*. Also verbatim in the Bodian interview. Filed under Essays with T.
3. **"The assumption that the egoless condition ... is a great mistake."** azquotes gives *The Path to No-Self* p.15 in one summary and *The Experience of No-Self* in another. Filed under Path (matches its preface argument that the unitive state is a 'hidden path' to no-self).
4. **"Instead of the usual unlocalized centre of myself, there was nothing there..."** azquotes: *The Path to No-Self* p.15. On content this is the famous opening of *The Experience of No-Self* (the discovery that 'self' was missing). Filed under ENS with T.
5. **"Only God is love, and for this love to be fully realized self must step aside..."** No aggregator gives a book; azquotes attaches a love/self quote to *The Experience of No-Self* p.155 but the pairing on the page is ambiguous. Filed under Path with T.
6. **Goodreads author page** mixes *What Is Self?* quotes (transformation passage) with the Christ/Buddha passage; several summaries warned that its per-quote book tags are unreliable.
7. **"Put another way, that which remains when there is no self is Christ in the Trinity for a Christian, but not for a Buddha."** One summary presented it as a reviewer's sentence; it may be Jeff Shore's foreword rather than Roberts. T.
8. **Dates.** *The Real Christ*: Wikipedia 2012 vs. publisher first printing Feb 2017 (ContemplativeChristians.com, 524 pp.). *Essays*: 2007 per josephbray citation.
