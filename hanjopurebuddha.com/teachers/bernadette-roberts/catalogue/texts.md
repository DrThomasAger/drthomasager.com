# Bernadette Roberts — texts catalogue

Bernadette Roberts (1931 – 27 November 2017), American Catholic contemplative, formerly a Discalced Carmelite (Alhambra, California, entered January 1949 at seventeen), author of *The Experience of No-Self*. Other people of the same name are excluded. Gathered 2026-10-25 by web search only (20 searches; the shared budget ran out before the second pass, see "Still open"). Machine-readable twin: `scratchpad/json-br/text.json` (62 items). Confidence: S = confirmed against a quoted copy or direct listing; T = secondary or recalled, wants checking; lead = location known, content unseen.

## Copyright and estate

**Who holds the rights.** Not established. Her later books carry the imprint "Contemplativechristians.com Publishing"; her website is run by friends (Ramesh Balakrishnan, set-up; Pat Masters, manager). No executor, literary estate or permissions contact was named in any result. For the trade editions the rights-holders of record are the publishers: SUNY Press (Experience 1993, Path 1991), Sentient Publications (What Is Self? 2005, The Real Christ 2016), Shambhala (1984/1985 printings).

**Copyright notices found** (as returned by the search tool from scanned copies; exact wording wants checking against the page images):

- *The Path to No-Self*, SUNY 1991 (from the pdfcoffee/archive copy): "Published by State University of New York Press, Albany © 1991 State University of New York All rights reserved". The notice allows only "brief quotations embodied in critical articles and reviews" and otherwise requires written permission from the publisher, whose address is given in the front matter. Note the copyright is in the name of the State University of New York, not the author.
- *What Is Self?*, Sentient Publications 2005 (from the Everand preview): marked copyright with all rights reserved; "may not be reproduced without permission, except for brief quotations embodied in critical articles and reviews" (paraphrase by the search tool).
- *Contemplative: Autobiography of the Early Years* (archive.org scan): copyright page shows "ISBN: 978-0-692-12517-5"; the copyright line itself was not returned.

**Her statements about reproduction and misattribution** (verbatim as returned):

- From her site (bernadetteroberts.blogspot.com / Bernadette's Friends): "there are no authorized videos or audio recordings of Bernadette on YouTube or any other site. Videos and audio recordings on these sites are either falsely attributed to her, violations of copyrights, and made available against her wishes."
- Her warning on *The Real Christ* (Books page; she "requested that no one order this book until they have read the warning below"): "Those who believe the man Jesus who walked this earth 2000 years ago was God, should read no further. Since I hold no human being is God, those who disagree will only find this book upsetting and disagreeable. This writing is not for those convinced they have the last word on Christ, but those searching for the real Christ."

No statement by her granting free reproduction of her writing was found; the evidence points the other way (ordinary copyright, and an explicit objection to unauthorised recordings). The archive.org and pdfcoffee scans therefore appear to be unauthorised.
